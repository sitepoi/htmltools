Personal Tax Preparation - Help

User help for Personal Tax Preparation. The single source of truth for how this tool works is its SSOT document (SSOT) - this file explains the same behavior for end users, in plain words. Generated from the tool code + SSOT + AI chat by scripts/generate-docs.js. Updated: 2026-09-21.

1. Overview

Personal Tax Preparation (MapleBooks Tax Prep BC) turns your bank statements, invoices and receipts into a clean income and expense ledger for your Canadian (BC) tax file, calculates GST and PST, and packs everything for your accountant.

It is for BC taxpayers with personal income and a small business - sole proprietors and one-person corporations - who want their books organized and their deductions found without manual spreadsheet work.

2. Before you start

  • The tool needs AI enabled (the CMS field "Enable AI Prompt Relay") to read documents, run the expense finder and chat with the tax assistant.
  • Upload needs "Enable File Upload" and "Enable File Content Extraction" turned on in the tool's CMS field settings.
  • Have your files ready: bank and credit-card statements, invoices you paid, invoices you sent, receipts, and T4 slips. PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, TXT and images are accepted.

3. Step by step

  1. Set up your profile. Open Settings, choose your filing entity (Personal / Sole Proprietor / Corporation), enter your business name and tax year, and set GST/PST registration.
  2. Add your documents. Open Documents and use the four areas: Bank statements, Invoices paid, Invoices sent and Other documents. Each upload is filed under its area and analyzed automatically.
  3. Review the ledger. Open Transactions and check every AI-extracted row. Fix a wrong category by editing it, or press "Re-classify with AI" to let the AI re-read the descriptions.
  4. Find missed deductions. Open Expense Finder and run the short interview (or the quick scan). Accept the suggestions that apply to you.
  5. Check GST / PST. Open the GST / PST page for your monthly and filing-period summaries, and fill the adjustment lines if needed.
  6. Hand off. Open Reports and export the Preparer Package (PDF) or email it to your accountant.

4. Common questions

QuestionAnswer
Where do my bank statements go?In the Documents page, under the Bank statements area. CSV files are filed there automatically.
What is the difference between invoices paid and invoices sent?Invoices paid are bills you paid - they become expenses. Invoices sent are the invoices you issued - they become income.
Are the AI numbers safe to file?The numbers are planning aids. Review every transaction and consider having an accountant review before submitting to the CRA.
Can I add a transaction by hand?Yes - the Transactions page has an "+ Add Transaction" button for manual entries.
What happens in read-only mode?You can view everything but cannot edit, upload or delete. The CMS controls this.
