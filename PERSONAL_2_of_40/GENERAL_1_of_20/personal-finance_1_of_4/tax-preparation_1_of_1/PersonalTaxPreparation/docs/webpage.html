Buyer questions

Is this for personal or company taxes?

Both. It handles personal income (T4), sole proprietors (T1 + T2125) and corporations (T2) in one file, with BC-specific GST and PST.

What if I am not GST registered?

Switch GST off in Settings and the tool tracks your books without it. When you register, the GST summary appears automatically.

Can I file directly from the tool?

The tool prepares the numbers and the package - the final filing stays with you or your accountant, exactly as recommended.
