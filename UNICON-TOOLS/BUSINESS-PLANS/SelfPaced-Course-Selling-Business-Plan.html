1. Executive summary

We sell self-paced exam preparation directly to individuals. The first product family is driving licence knowledge tests in Canada and the United States, followed by national certifications such as the Canadian boat licence, then high-stakes exams including SAT, MCAT, LSAT and IB.

The product is a mastery system, not a video library: lesson quizzes with five rotating sets of ten questions, section exams, a closed fifty-question readiness exam, weak-area drilling and a downloadable completion record. The commercial differentiator is a money-back guarantee that only the readiness exam can qualify, which turns the course into a verified readiness signal.

Acquisition is search-led: one hub and 25 to 30 supporting pages per course, a free ten-question sample test that captures email, short video, and individual creator affiliates. Paid advertising starts only after organic conversion is proven.

Production runs at one complete course per month for twelve months, then two per month, then three to four as templates, AI assistance and paid expert reviewers come online. The long-term inventory target is more than one hundred courses.

  guarantee:   { enabled: true, requiresAllSections: true, requiresFinalExam: true, claimWindowDays: 90 }

Rule that cannot be broken. The readiness exam must never reuse questions the learner practised. If it does, the certificate proves memorisation, the guarantee becomes a lottery and the business loses its only real differentiator.

3.2 The guarantee

Complete every lesson, pass every lesson quiz at 80% or higher, pass every section exam at 80% or higher, and pass the final readiness exam at 80% or higher. If you then fail your official knowledge test on your first attempt within 90 days of purchase, we refund the full course price. One refund per person. Written knowledge test only, not the road test. A photo of the official result is required.

3.3 Product requirements

  1. A course must be jurisdiction-accurate, verified against the current official source, with the review date visible on every course page.
  2. Lesson quizzes use five sets of ten questions, at 80% to progress, with a 15-minute study wait after a fail.
  3. Every section ends with a 20 to 25 question section exam at 80%.
  4. Every course ends with a closed 50-question readiness exam at 80%, from a bank the learner has not practised, with no answer reveal.
  5. The completion record carries the rules version and is downloadable.
  6. Assessment rules and thresholds are configurable per course, never hardcoded per jurisdiction.

One domain, one brand, flat short paths. Every jurisdiction uses the same page template. Thirty pages per course, added to the internal link graph, is the compounding asset.

  • Cite the official handbook and show "reviewed against the current handbook" with a visible date.
  • Never imply government affiliation. No official logos, no government names in the brand.
  • Every question page shows the explanation, not just the answer.
  • Structured data: FAQPage and Course where valid. Never fake review schema.
  • Mobile-first: most of this audience arrives on a phone.

6.3 Free sample test funnel

  1. Free 10-question test, no login. Instant score, one explanation, single call to action to see the course.
  2. Optional email for the full study plan. Seven genuinely useful emails across fourteen days, each teaching one topic.
  3. Offer at the point of demonstrated need. The sequence ends with the readiness exam and the guarantee, not a discount.

6.4 Video plan

  • Ten clips per course, 30 to 45 seconds each: question on screen, answer, one-sentence explanation.
  • Formats: can you pass this question, most people get this wrong, three signs you will fail.
  • Post to Shorts, Reels and TikTok. Link in bio to the free test, never to a checkout.
  • Reuse the same clips as embedded media on topic pages after 60 days.

6.5 Affiliate and creator program

  • 30% commission, 60-day cookie, paid monthly, individuals only.
  • Target driving instructors, student creators and education micro-influencers.
  • Give each affiliate a free guaranteed seat so they can speak from experience.
  • Never let affiliates bid on brand terms or use official-sounding claims.

6.6 Paid advertising rules

  • Do not start before organic conversion is at or above 2%.
  • Acquisition cost cap: 25% of average order value, roughly $22 on an $89 product.
  • Test budget: $300-500 per channel per month, one channel at a time.
  • Kill a channel after 100 clicks with zero sales.

7.3 Expert review model from year 2

  • Tier A - founder built: driving, motorcycle, boat, dangerous goods. 50 to 60 hours each.
  • Tier B - expert reviewed: SAT and IB. Founder drafts, a subject teacher reviews for $500-1,500 per course.
  • Tier C - expert authored: MCAT and LSAT. Paid specialist author with revenue share or flat fee, because credibility is the product.

7.4 Quality gate before publishing

  • Every question verified against the current official source, with the source date recorded.
  • Second review pass on every question, separate from the authoring pass.
  • No question duplicated between the practice pool and the final exam bank.
  • Every explanation is at least one sentence and names the rule, not just the answer.
  • Assessment configuration matches the course category and the guarantee terms.
  • Launch kit complete: 25+ pages, free test, videos, email sequence, internal links.
  • Completion record, refund terms and rules version tested end to end.

17. Risks and mitigations

Every risk of this plan, linked to its phase. Review every open risk at the monthly review: solve it (tick the checkbox) or accept it with a reason and a date.

Risks: 0 open - 0 solved - 0 accepted (0% solved)
R-01 Content error reaches a paying learner

Applies to: every course, especially phase 1 onward.

What can go wrong: a wrong answer teaches the wrong rule, the learner fails, and the guarantee plus reviews are at risk.

Likelihood / impact: medium likelihood, high impact.

Mitigation: handbook verification, a second review pass, visible review date, 24-hour correction process, refund on request.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-02 Readiness exam reuses practised questions

Applies to: T-07, T-08.

What can go wrong: the certificate proves memorisation, the pass rate is overstated and the guarantee becomes uninsurable.

Likelihood / impact: low likelihood if designed correctly, critical impact.

Mitigation: separate exam bank, automated check that no exam item appears in the practice pool, release blocked otherwise.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-03 Search ranking slower than planned

Applies to: every launch kit.

What can go wrong: pages index but do not rank, revenue stays near zero for six months, and production confidence collapses.

Likelihood / impact: medium likelihood, high impact.

Mitigation: long-tail page strategy, short video as a second channel, email list ownership, paid tests only after organic proof.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-04 Guarantee terms not tied to a rules version

Applies to: T-02, T-03, T-08.

What can go wrong: rules change after purchase and a buyer is judged under terms they never agreed to, creating a consumer-protection dispute.

Likelihood / impact: medium likelihood, high impact.

Mitigation: snapshot the rules version at purchase, show it in the receipt and the completion record, judge refunds under that version.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-05 Refund rate above plan

Applies to: the guarantee tier.

What can go wrong: refunds exceed 12% for two months and the guarantee becomes a loss maker.

Likelihood / impact: medium likelihood, medium impact.

Mitigation: readiness gate before eligibility, refund deflection offer first, 48-hour refunds to avoid chargebacks, monthly reserve review.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-06 Production cadence slips and launch kits are dropped

Applies to: phases 2 to 4.

What can go wrong: courses ship without pages, sales never arrive, and the portfolio becomes dead inventory.

Likelihood / impact: high likelihood, high impact.

Mitigation: course and kit ship as one unit; a month without a kit does not count as a shipped course; section 15 tracks both.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-07 US sales tax and payment handling

Applies to: T-16, T-17.

What can go wrong: uncollected tax and processor account issues across four states.

Likelihood / impact: medium likelihood, medium impact.

Mitigation: decide processor versus merchant of record before the first US page goes live; document the choice in section 13.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-08 Support load consumes production time

Applies to: phase 5.

What can go wrong: contacts rise above 10 per 100 sales, production stops, and the monthly cadence fails.

Likelihood / impact: medium likelihood, high impact.

Mitigation: help centre, canned answers, AI assistant, tracked metric, marketing pause when the metric breaches.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-09 High-ticket content lacks credibility

Applies to: T-19, T-21.

What can go wrong: SAT, MCAT and LSAT buyers do not trust an unknown brand written without a named expert.

Likelihood / impact: high likelihood, medium impact.

Mitigation: paid subject reviewer with a public credential, expert byline, sample lessons, money-back guarantee.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open

R-10 Copycats reuse the question bank

Applies to: all content.

What can go wrong: a competitor scrapes and republishes the questions.

Likelihood / impact: medium likelihood, low impact.

Mitigation: terms of use, no bulk export, copyright notice, and speed of expansion as the real defence.

Last review: 2026-11-16 - open
