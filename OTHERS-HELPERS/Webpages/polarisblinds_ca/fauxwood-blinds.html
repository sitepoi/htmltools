Benefits of fauxwood blinds

All the warmth, none of the upkeep

Fauxwood blinds offer a versatile and stylish solution for light control and privacy, in rooms where real wood would struggle.

01

Durability

Unlike real wood, fauxwood resists moisture, warping and cracking - perfect for high-humidity areas like bathrooms and kitchens.

02

Affordability

More cost-effective than real wood, offering a high-end look without the high-end price tag.

03

Easy maintenance

A simple wipe with a damp cloth is usually sufficient to keep them looking fresh and new.

04

Versatility

Available in a variety of colours, finishes and sizes, fauxwood blinds complement any decor - traditional to contemporary.

05

UV protection

Help protect your furniture and flooring from harmful UV rays, reducing the risk of fading and damage.

06

Energy efficiency

The slats help insulate your windows - cooler in summer, warmer in winter, and kinder to your energy bills.
