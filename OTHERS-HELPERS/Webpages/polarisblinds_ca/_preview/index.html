Questions, answered

Before you ask us

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. We visit your home, measure the windows and bring samples at no charge, with no obligation to order.

Do you install the blinds?

Yes. Our service covers measure, fitting and installation, so the finish is exactly right.

Can I get cordless or motorized options?

Yes. Cordless designs are available for child-safe homes, and motorized operation is offered on several collections.

Which areas do you serve?

Calgary and the surrounding area: Airdrie, Chestermere, Cochrane, Okotoks, High River and Strathmore.
