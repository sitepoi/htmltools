Why roman blinds

A tailored jacket for your window

Roman blinds combine the neatness of a blind with the softness of fabric - a classic choice that never dates.

01

Soft fabric folds

Flat when lowered, gently folded when raised - roman blinds keep their shape wash after wash.

02

Endless fabric choices

Linen, cotton blends, textures and patterns - find a fabric that belongs to your room.

03

Gentle light control

Unlined fabrics glow softly in daylight; lined options dim the room for restful evenings.

04

Classic elegance

A clean, tailored look that suits traditional and modern interiors alike.

05

Privacy with warmth

One full panel of fabric screens your room while it adds a layer of softness and warmth to the wall.

06

Easy care

Regular dusting and gentle spot cleaning keep roman blinds looking their best.

In your home

The soft layer every room wants

Pair roman blinds with long curtains for a layered, designer look - or let them stand alone for a clean finish. They dress bedrooms, dining rooms and bay windows beautifully.

  • Neat, even folds when raised
  • One elegant panel when lowered
  • Cordless options for family homes
  • Lined options for extra warmth and darkening
