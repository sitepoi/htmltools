02 Finals memories

The days the cups were decided

Every season closes with an incredible day of action at Hjorth Road Park. These are the finals the community still talks about.

Prem 1 Sabeto FC prevailed over LAFC in an instant classic

The Premier Division 1 final had everything. Golden Glove Jayden Mahraj and coach Zoheb Nasim led Sabeto FC to the cup.

Prem 2 Rain or Shine All Stars completed the double

After winning the Prem 3 regular season, they were promoted and triumphed over the White Falcons in the Prem 2 playoff final.

Div 1b Syria FC captured their double

Winners of the Prem 2 regular season, Syria FC added the 1b playoff final over the newly formed Muslim Food Bank team.

2021 A season to remember

Sabeto FC, Etihad FC, Syria 35 and LAFC lifted the 2021 cups across Premier Div 1, Premier Div 2, Masters and U18.
