01 The divisions

A division for every stage of the game

Every division plays a regular season followed by playoffs. Champions are promoted and honoured on finals day.

P1 Top flight

Premier Division 1

The highest level in the league. The fastest football, the toughest finals, and the trophy every team wants.

  • Regular season + playoff final
  • League honours for every final
  • Champions hold the Prem 1 title
P2 Competitive

Premier Division 2

Strong, organized football one step below the top flight, and the direct promotion route for teams chasing the double.

  • Regular season + playoff final
  • Promotion to Prem 1
  • Division 1b final pathway
P3 Biggest field

Premier Division 3

Our biggest division, split into 3A and 3B groups. New teams start here, and champions earn promotion to Prem 2.

  • 3A and 3B playoff finals
  • Promotion to Prem 2
  • Perfect entry point for new clubs
M Veterans

Masters Division

The 35+ league for players who still have game. Smart football, great rivalries, and a finals day of its own.

  • Players 35 and over
  • Regular season + final
  • Season champions every year
U18 Rising stars

U18 Division

The next generation of the league. Young players develop, compete for their own final, and grow into the open divisions.

  • Players under 18
  • Development and competition
  • Best Young Player honours

02 How a season works

From kickoff to the cup

1

Regular season

Every team plays a full fixture list across its division. The regular season title is its own trophy.

2

Playoffs and finals

The season ends at Hjorth Road Park with playoff finals across every division, from Prem 1 down to 3B.

3

Honours and promotion

Champions lift the cup and earn promotion. The league honours its best players, coaches and managers.

Questions, answered

Soccer, quickly explained

Which division should I join?

New clubs usually start in Premier Division 3, and new players are placed by the association after registration. If you already have a team, tell us in the membership form.

How long is a season?

Every season runs a full fixture list, playoffs, and finals day. The exact calendar is announced by the association before each season.

Can my whole team join together?

Yes. Team managers can register a full squad through the membership form or by contacting the association directly.

Are there awards beyond the titles?

Yes - every season the league honours the Golden Glove, Best Coach/Manager, Best Young Player and Team MVPs from the finals.
