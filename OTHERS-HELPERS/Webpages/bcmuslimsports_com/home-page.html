Questions, answered

Before you ask us

Who can join the association?

Players of all ages, from our U18 division up to the Masters. Teams and individual players are both welcome to register.

Where do the leagues play?

Our seasons and finals are played at Hjorth Road Park in Surrey, British Columbia.

How do I register for a season?

Fill in the membership form and the association will get back to you with division placement and season details.

Is there hockey too?

Yes. Our hockey program runs alongside the soccer leagues and welcomes every skill level. See the hockey page for more.

Can I volunteer, coach or manage a team?

Absolutely - our coaches and managers are the heart of the league. Reach out through the contact page and we will put you in touch with a team.
