Questions, answered

Hockey, quickly explained

When does the hockey season run?

The hockey calendar is announced by the association before each season. Register your interest and you will hear the details first.

Do I need experience to join?

No. The program welcomes every skill level, including players who are new to the ice. Placement makes sure games stay fun and fair.

Can I register a full hockey team?

Yes. Team registration works the same way as our soccer leagues - register through the membership form or contact the association.
