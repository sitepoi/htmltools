One community.
One game at a time.

BC Muslim Sports Association runs soccer leagues, a hockey program and community tournaments for Muslim families across British Columbia - organized by volunteers who love the game and the community it builds.

Our mission

Bringing the ummah together one game at a time

That line is not just our tagline - it is the whole idea. The league grows more competitive each year, but it stays built on brotherhood between clubs, family match days, and a place for every age group, from U18 to Masters.

From regular seasons to playoff finals at Hjorth Road Park, from the Mosque Cup to the FANCA Over 42 gold medals, everything we run serves one goal: a stronger community through sport.

The board

Volunteers who keep the league running

The association is run by volunteers from the community. These are the roles that carry the seasons - from fixtures to finals day.

President

Leads the association, chairs the board, and keeps the vision of the league moving forward season after season.

Vice President

Supports the president, steps in when needed, and helps the association run smoothly across its sports.

Secretary

Keeps the records, the communication and the paperwork that hold the association together.

Treasurer

Looks after the association's finances - registrations, donations and the budget that books the pitches.

Directors at Large

The hands-on volunteers who run divisions, coordinate team managers, and make match days happen.
