Transparency

You should know where your money goes

CentreCanada Newcomer Services Society is a community-based, volunteer-driven organization. Donations support services for newcomers across British Columbia — and campaign donations stay with the campaign they were given to.

Need a receipt or have a question about a gift? Email hello@centrecanada.org.

  • 0% platform fee Donations run through Zeffy, which does not charge the charity a platform fee.
  • 100% to the campaign Gifts to The Common Table campaign are directed to that campaign.
  • Volunteer-driven Volunteers donate thousands of hours a year alongside every dollar given.
