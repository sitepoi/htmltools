Our story

Empowering new beginnings in Canada

As individuals embark on the exciting journey to a new country, they carry dreams of building enriched, inclusive and innovative communities. Those dreams are not just personal aspirations — they are essential building blocks for a diverse and thriving Canada.

Moving to a new country can be daunting, filled with uncertainties and questions. That is why CentreCanada serves as a reliable source of information, constantly updated to give newcomers the latest insights and guidance. Our multilingual website bridges language barriers and promotes inclusivity.

Each newcomer has unique requirements, from professional guidance to everyday necessities. Whether you need help with employment, housing, healthcare or education, our team is here to support you every step of the way.

Our mission

To ease the transition process for newcomers — ensuring the journey is as smooth as possible, and that no one has to navigate it alone.

At the heart of our mission is the belief that newcomers bring invaluable contributions to the Canadian tapestry. Their diverse backgrounds, skills and experiences enrich our communities, making them more vibrant and dynamic — integral parts of the social, cultural and economic fabric of Canada.

Governance

A society governed by its members

CentreCanada Newcomer Services Society is governed by a Board of Directors. The Board consists of 11 directors serving standard three-year terms, with a one-time optional one-year extension. Directors may not serve consecutive terms without a three-year hiatus.

Voting members may nominate candidates for the Board. Candidates must meet eligibility requirements under the Society’s Bylaws and the BC Societies Act. Up to two individuals from non-member backgrounds may be nominated by a Voting Member.
