Enrolling Your Child
in School

Primary or high school — the steps are similar. Find your catchment school, bring the right documents, and register with the school district. Here is what to expect.

Education guide Approx. 5 min read CentreCanada team

Public education in British Columbia is organized by school district. Your address decides which district and which school your child is entitled to attend.

1. Find your school district and catchment school

Each district has a boundary map or a school locator tool. Enter your home address and it will show your catchment school — the school your child would normally attend. If you are still choosing where to live, check the school attached to an address before you sign a lease.

  • Burnaby — School District 41
  • New Westminster — School District 40
  • Greater Victoria — School District 61

2. Gather the documents

Exact requirements vary by district, but newcomer families are usually asked for:

  • Proof of the child's age, such as a birth certificate or passport
  • Proof of address, such as a lease, utility bill or bank statement
  • Immigration documents for the child and parent or guardian
  • Immunization records, if available
  • Previous school records or report cards, translated if needed
  • Proof of guardianship if the child is not living with both parents

If a document is missing, register anyway and explain the situation. Districts can tell you what they will accept while you wait for the original to arrive.

Do not wait for perfect paperwork

Children can usually start school while documents are being completed. Talking to the district office early is almost always better than waiting until everything is in hand.

3. Register

Registration usually happens at the school itself or at a district welcome centre. Bring the originals and a copy of each document. Kindergarten registration often opens early in the calendar year for the following September — check your district's dates, because they differ.

For high school, the school will usually review transcripts to place your child in the right courses and grade.

4. What happens after you register

  • Your child may be assessed for English language support (ELL)
  • The school will confirm the start date and any supplies needed
  • You will be asked for emergency contacts and medical information
  • Bus, meal and before-and-after-school care options can be discussed

5. Stay connected once school starts

Schools communicate through email, apps and paper notices. Tell the school which language you prefer for important conversations, and ask about interpretation if you need it. Attending parent-teacher meetings early helps you understand how your child is settling in.

This guide is general information. Registration windows, catchment rules and required documents are set by each school district — confirm the details with your district office. CentreCanada can help you find the right contact and prepare documents.
