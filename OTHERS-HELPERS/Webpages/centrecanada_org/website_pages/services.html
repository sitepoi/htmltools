01 · Department

Documentation Assistance

Newcomers usually deal with lots of paperwork and applications. CentreCanada will provide documentation assistance — helping you understand what is being asked, which documents you need, and how to submit them correctly.

  • Understanding forms, letters and application requirements
  • Organising supporting documents before submission
  • Guidance on where to file and what to expect next
Request documentation help →
02 · Department

Educational Assistance

Starting education from scratch or improving careers through courses will be provided by CentreCanada. From school enrolment for children to adult learning and language programs, we connect families with the right opportunities.

  • School enrolment guidance for children and youth
  • Language and skills course referrals
  • Our own Weekend School and Summer School programs
Explore the Learning Centre →
03 · Department

Employment Assistance

Our staff will assist newcomers to build up their careers utilising the resources provided by local and government authorities — from understanding local job expectations to finding the programs that move a career forward.

  • Resume and job-search guidance
  • Referrals to employment and training resources
  • Career seminars and employer connections
See career resources →
04 · Department

Food & Clothing

Knowing how to reach specific food and essential clothing is another challenge. CentreCanada will assist newcomers in finding trusted local resources so basic needs are met while you find your footing.

  • Referrals to food support and community kitchens
  • Essential clothing resources for families
  • Seasonal drives and community deliveries
Ask for food & clothing help →
05 · Department

Housing Aid

Along with temporary accommodation assistance, newcomers will be informed about housing possibilities in their neighbourhoods — what to look for, what to ask and how to avoid common pitfalls.

  • Temporary accommodation guidance on arrival
  • Neighbourhood housing information
  • Practical renting tips before you sign
Read our renting guide →
06 · Department

Integration

Understanding each other and shaping a new life in a new community may be stressful. CentreCanada will devote itself to making integration possible through dialogue, with the help of its members and volunteers.

  • Dialogue circles and community conversations
  • Events that connect newcomers and neighbours
  • Volunteer and member pathways into the community
