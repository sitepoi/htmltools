About us

New to Canada?
You are not alone.

CentreCanada Newcomer Services Society is a beacon of hope for newcomers arriving in Canada. Moving to a new country can be daunting and filled with uncertainties — our organisation is committed to easing the transition so your journey is as smooth as possible.

We are a reliable, constantly updated source of information with a multilingual team, and we provide services tailored to each newcomer’s unique needs — from employment and housing to healthcare and education.

  • Culturally responsive, multilingual support
  • Practical help with everyday paperwork
  • A welcoming community for the whole family

How it works

From first hello to settled in

01

Reach out

Send us a request online or drop by one of our offices in Burnaby, New Westminster or Victoria.

02

Meet our team

We listen first. A multilingual team member learns your situation and what matters most right now.

03

Get a plan

Together we map the steps — documents, school, work, housing — with clear next actions.

04

Settle in

We stay with you through follow-ups, programs and community events until you feel at home.
