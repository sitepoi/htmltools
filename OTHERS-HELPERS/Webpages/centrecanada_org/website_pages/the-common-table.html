The story

A fire, a question, a table

A fire at Pinecrest Manor left neighbours without a home. For newcomers and long-time residents alike, losing a place to live means losing documents, routines and the small things that make a neighbourhood feel safe.

The young people in our programs did not wait for someone else to act. They asked how they could help — then they planned an evening that would bring the whole community to one table and turn that question into something concrete.

That is the spirit of The Common Table: nobody sits outside the circle because they arrived late, spoke a different language or lost everything.

Local performers gave their time so the night could be joyful as well as meaningful.

A silent auction

Donated items and experiences from local businesses turned generosity into pledges.

Artwork by students

Summer school students contributed artwork, showing what community looks like through their eyes.

Youth organizers

Volunteers planned, promoted and ran the evening — the same young people who asked how to help.
