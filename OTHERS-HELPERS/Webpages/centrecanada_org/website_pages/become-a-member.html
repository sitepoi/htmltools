Membership is for people who want to be part of the work — not just a mailing list.

What membership gives you

A seat at the table

A vote at the AGM

Voting members approve the direction of the society and nominate candidates for the board.

A real community

Events, programs and gatherings where newcomers and long-time residents build lasting ties.

Governance transparency

An 11-director board elected for three-year terms, with a three-year hiatus after each term.

Service with purpose

Your hours go straight into newcomer support, education and community programs.

How it works

Membership is earned through service

CentreCanada is a volunteer-driven society. Members pledge at least 50 volunteer hours per year, and that commitment is what makes the work possible.
