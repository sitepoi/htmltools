Community Health Worker (CHW)

Part-time · Hybrid

Lead outreach and education in your community, organise community forums, deliver health literacy workshops and bridge the gap between service providers and diverse communities. Fluency in Dari/Pashto, Arabic, Spanish, Tigrinya or Turkish is an asset, along with a medical or health-related background.

Location British Columbia — hybrid remote & in-person Rate $23.62 – $28.10 per hour, based on experience

English Development Instructor

Contract · In-person

Plan and deliver beginner-level English lessons focused on everyday communication, create a supportive environment for senior learners, prepare simple lesson materials and track weekly attendance. ESL teaching or adult education experience is an asset.

Location Burnaby, BC Schedule 12 weeks · 2 sessions per week · approx. 45 hours Rate $22 per hour

Digital Literacy Instructor

Contract · In-person

Teach basic digital skills — smartphone use, internet browsing, email, video calls, online safety and common apps — with patience and hands-on support for learners at different paces.

Location Burnaby, BC Schedule 12 weeks · 2 sessions per week · approx. 45 hours
