Frequently asked questions

Cannot find your question? Send it to us — a real person will answer.

Ask a question
Who can use CentreCanada services?

Newcomers arriving in Canada — individuals and families. We serve communities across British Columbia from our offices in Burnaby, New Westminster and Victoria.

Do I need to speak English to get help?

No. Our team and website are multilingual. Tell us the language you prefer when you request a service and we will do our best to match you with the right team member.

How do I request a service?

Complete the request form on the Request a Service page, call or visit an office, or email hello@centrecanada.org. You do not need to know which department you need — we will route your request.

Is there a cost for services?

Most information and referrals are provided by the society at no charge. Some programs (for example, Weekend School) have set fees. Ask us about your situation and we will explain any cost before you proceed.

Can I volunteer or become a member?

Yes. Volunteers commit to at least 50 hours per year, and membership is available through the volunteer registration process. See the Become a Volunteer and Become a Member pages.

How can I donate?

You can give one-time or monthly through the Donate page. Campaigns such as The Common Table send 100% of donations to the families affected by the New Westminster fire.
