The commitment

50hours / year

Volunteers pledge at least 50 hours per year. That commitment is also the pathway to membership in the society.

Ways to help

Your time, matched to what matters to you

Events & festivals

Help set up, welcome guests and run activities at iftars, picnics, career festivals and city events.

Children & learning

Support Weekend School, Summer School and camps with classroom help and activities.

Newcomer support

Assist with documentation, food and clothing drives, and welcome visits for new families.

Dialogue & integration

Host conversations and connect newcomers with neighbours, members and local partners.

Why volunteer

More than hours on a form

Volunteering with CentreCanada is a way into the community — for newcomers and long-time residents alike. You will meet people, build skills and see the direct impact of your time.

  • BelongingA team that celebrates your contribution
  • ExperienceEvents, programs and community services
  • Membership pathway50 pledged hours leads to membership
  • FlexibilityMatch your hours to your availability
