Volunteer role

Online maths teacher

Teach the weekly free online maths lesson: explain the topic, work through it with the group, and help the students who are stuck before the next lesson.

  • 1 hour a week
  • Online
  • Training provided

What you do

  • Prepare the topic before the lesson, using the lesson plan we give you.
  • Teach the group online for one hour a week.
  • Explain the same idea a second way when someone is stuck.
  • Keep the group practising on the work their class is doing at school.
  • Tell us honestly where the gap was, so the next lesson can close it.

What you need

  • Comfortable teaching maths from grade 5 to grade 12.
  • One fixed weekly slot you can hold.
  • Patience with a student who does not get it the first time.
  • Enough comfort with an online meeting to share your screen.

How hours work

1 hour a week - at least one lesson every week

Plus a short preparation before each lesson. You choose the slot.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says what you taught
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role.
  2. Short interview, then a teaching sample: you explain a topic you know well for about ten minutes as if we were the students, and we ask the questions a real student would ask.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
