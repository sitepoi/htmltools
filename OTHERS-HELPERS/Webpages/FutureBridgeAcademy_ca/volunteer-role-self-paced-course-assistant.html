Volunteer role

Self-paced course assistant

Guide a group of students through a self-paced course, online in a WhatsApp group: answer their questions, watch how they are moving through the subjects, and open the next subject when they are ready for it.

  • Online
  • WhatsApp group
  • Your own schedule
Apply for this role All volunteer roles

What you do

  • Answer student questions in the group chat.
  • Supervise how each student is moving through the course, subject by subject.
  • Accept a student for the next subject once the current one is finished.
  • Check on the students who have gone quiet for a while.
  • Tell the team when a subject or an explanation is not working.

What you need

  • Quick to reply on WhatsApp.
  • Comfortable using a course system, and willing to be shown where to look.
  • Patient: the same question comes twice, in different words.
  • Careful with student information.

Time required

Online in a WhatsApp group, on your own schedule

You answer between your own commitments, with the students' course dates in mind.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says what you did
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample: we show you a student's progress and you tell us what you would reply and what you would unlock next.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
