Volunteer role

Career coach

Guide one high-school student at a time: set the goal together, build their file, and work the tasks until the file is complete and they are ready to apply - or ready for the next step.

  • One-to-one
  • High-school students
  • Online
Apply for this role All volunteer roles

What you do

  • Set the goal with the student, not for them.
  • Open their file and agree the tasks that will complete it.
  • Meet the student regularly and work those tasks with them.
  • Keep the file moving with dates the student actually agreed to.
  • Say when the goal needs to change, and prepare the student for the next step either way.

What you need

  • You have walked a university, college or trades path yourself.
  • Comfortable keeping a teenager honest about deadlines without nagging.
  • Reliable at replying, because your student is waiting on you.
  • Straight with families about what is realistic.

Time required

Hours agreed with the programme lead

Set by how many students you take, and agreed with you before you start.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says who you guided
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample: we describe one student's situation and you outline the goal, the first five tasks and what would make you change the plan.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
