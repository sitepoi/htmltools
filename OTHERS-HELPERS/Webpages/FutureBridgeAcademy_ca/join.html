Before you send it

What we will and will not do with your details

  • 📧
    Only lesson contact

    We use your email to tell you when the next lesson has space and to send the joining details. Nothing else, and no list sold or shared.

  • 🔒
    Only what we need

    A parent's name, the student's name, an email, a grade and a subject, plus an optional topic. No school names, no documents.

  • ✋
    Leaving is easy

    One message and you are off the list, with everything deleted. No exit interview, no reminder sequence.
