Volunteer role

Mock exam organiser

Run a monthly exam with the exam team, from printing the papers and managing the registrations to checking the answers and sending the results to the families.

  • 6 to 8 hours per exam
  • At most one exam every 2 months
  • In person on exam day

What you do

  • Print and prepare the exam papers before the day.
  • Manage the registrations online: the list, the confirmations, the reminders.
  • Help in the classroom on exam day: set up, hand out, keep the timing, collect the papers.
  • Check the collected answers against the answer key.
  • Enter the results into the system.
  • Email and WhatsApp the parents and students with their results.

What you need

  • Organised with dates, lists and follow-ups.
  • Comfortable working in a registration system (we show you how).
  • Free on the exam day you take.
  • Careful with other people's results.

How hours work

About 6 to 8 hours per exam

Measured across the days around the exam, and never more than one exam every two months.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says what you ran
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample: talk us through how you would run one exam day, from the morning set-up to sending the results.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
