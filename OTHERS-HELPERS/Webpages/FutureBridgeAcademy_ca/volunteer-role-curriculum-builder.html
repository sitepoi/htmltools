Volunteer role

Curriculum builder

Build the material one atomic task at a time: take a task from our list, finish it properly, mark it done and take the next. How much you take on is completely up to you.

  • From 1 hour a week
  • Your own pace
  • Remote
Apply for this role All volunteer roles

What you do

  • Take one task at a time from our long task list.
  • Write or check the material that task asks for.
  • Keep the quality the same as the tasks around yours.
  • Mark the task done and take the next one whenever you have time.
  • Ask when a task is unclear, instead of guessing.

What you need

  • Solid in the subject you want to build for.
  • Able to write accurate material, not just fix what is already there.
  • Comfortable working from a list, with no one chasing you.
  • Careful with detail: a wrong answer in a worksheet reaches students.

Time required

Completely up to you - from 1 hour a week

More if you want it. The task list is long, so you never run out of work.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that names the material you built
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role, saying which subject you want to build for.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample: we send you one real task from the list and you complete it.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
