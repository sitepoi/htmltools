Volunteer role

Scholarship seminar presenter (English)

Find the scholarships that are really open, put every detail into our system, build the pages of a shared presentation - then present them in English so students and parents know what is real, what the deadline is and what to prepare before it closes.

  • Research and presenting
  • English
  • Online seminars
Apply for this role All volunteer roles

What you do

  • Research the scholarships that are open now or opening soon, from official sources only.
  • Add each one to our system as a record with every detail that matters: value, eligibility, required documents, deadlines, essay or reference requirements, and the official link.
  • Build the pages of our shared presentation from those records: one scholarship per page, in the order families have to act on them.
  • Present the seminar in English and answer questions, including the awkward ones about money and eligibility.
  • Keep the records current: when a deadline or a criterion changes, the record and its page change with it.

Two parts, one role. The research and data work, the presenting work, or both - you agree with the programme lead which part you take. Some volunteers start with the records and move to presenting later, and some only ever prepare the data, which we value just as much.

What you need

  • For the research part: patient, precise reading, and the habit of checking the official page rather than a summary.
  • For the presenting part: comfortable presenting online - our seminars run as video calls, often to a large audience of students and parents at once.
  • English at a level where nothing gets lost.
  • Careful: a wrong deadline in a record or on a slide can cost a student a year.

Time required

Hours agreed with the programme lead

Research is self paced and can be done in small blocks. Presenting hours are agreed and you are asked before you are put on a seminar date.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says what you presented
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role, saying whether you want the research and data part, the presenting part, or both.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample: either one scholarship added as a complete record, or a ten minute seminar outline on one scholarship - whichever part you want to do.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
