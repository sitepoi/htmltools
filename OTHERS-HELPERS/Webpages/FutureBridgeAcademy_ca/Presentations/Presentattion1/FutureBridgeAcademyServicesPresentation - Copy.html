New service 1 of 3 Self-Paced Courses

A curriculum becomes a path

Built for the student who wants to move faster, catch up, or keep learning between live lessons.

  • A course is written as sections; each section holds lessons; sections unlock in order.
  • The student works at their own pace against a recommended end date.
  • A supervisor follows progress and approves work - no spreadsheets, no chasing.
  • Every quiz answer, status and setting is kept in one saved record for that student.

The course content is authored once. Each student's record is separate, so changing the course never erases a student's progress.

New service 3 of 3 English Article Writing

Write, get scored, improve

One focused writing surface for one essay, with rubric-based evaluation that never rewrites the student's work.

  • The teacher defines the assignment: subject, instructions, minimum word count and rubrics.
  • The student writes, presses Evaluate My Essay, and gets a score plus concrete next steps.
  • Teachers can add rubrics - up to eight - and every class is judged the same way.
  • The student makes every change. The tool never edits the essay.

The 100-point rubric

Content & Ideas30
Organization & Structure20
Evidence & Argumentation20
Language & Style15
Grammar & Mechanics15

Five consistent criteria beat a vague "good essay" comment every time - and the score is feedback, not a replacement for the writing.
