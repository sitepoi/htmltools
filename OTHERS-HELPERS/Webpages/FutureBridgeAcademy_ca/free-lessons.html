What a lesson looks like

Small steps, on the topic your child is already studying

Nobody is set loose on a random worksheet. Every lesson starts where the school class is this week and ends with something specific to work on.

01

Join from any device

One link, a normal browser, nothing to install. A phone, a tablet or a computer with internet is enough.

02

Warm up on last week

A short recap of the previous lesson, so gaps from the week before are caught before they turn into habits.

03

Work on the school topic

The main part of the lesson covers what the class is doing at school right now, explained at the level your child actually needs.

04

Practise with a mentor there

Students work while mentors watch, so a wrong turn is corrected in the moment instead of becoming next month's confusion.

05

Leave with the next step

Every lesson ends with what to practise before the next one, in plain words the child can read back to you.

Who it is for

If your child is in grades 4 to 12, there is a place here

Our families arrive in very different places, and that is the point. The lesson covers the same topic for everyone, while the practice sets come at more than one level.

  • 🌱
    A student who is behind

    Smaller steps, the same topic, and no pretending to understand something that has not clicked yet.

  • 📈
    A student doing fine

    Depth instead of repetition: harder questions on the same topic, and habits that hold up under exam pressure.

  • 🌐
    A family new to Canada

    Parents learn how the system works at the same time as their child learns the maths.

  • 🏆
    A student who wants more

    Contest maths and contest preparation run as their own separate track. The free lesson stays the foundation, never the ceiling.

What you need

Nothing to buy, nothing to install

  • A phone, tablet or computer with internet
  • A quiet corner for the lesson
  • Paper and a pencil
  • The topic the class is on right now, if you know it

A camera and microphone are helpful but never required. Reading and asking questions in the chat works just as well.

Questions parents ask

The honest answers

Are the online lessons really free?

Yes. The weekly online lessons are free and always will be. The monthly mock exam, the seminars and the community are free too. When something is not free, we say so before you agree to anything.

Do we have to come every week?

No. Come when you can. Every lesson works on the topic the class is on, so a missed week is a gap, not a disaster, and the practice set from that week still helps.

Is it only maths?

Mathematics runs now. English and writing opens with the centre, and families in the community hear about it first. Ask us and we will tell you exactly what is running this month.

My child is behind. Will they feel out of place?

Being behind is the normal reason families come to us, and nobody is compared to anyone else. The topic is the same for the group, while practice comes at more than one level so everyone has something they can finish and something they can reach for.

How do we hear about the next lesson?

Dates are shared with the community before each lesson cycle, and you can always ask through the contact page. Send one message and we will keep you on the list.
