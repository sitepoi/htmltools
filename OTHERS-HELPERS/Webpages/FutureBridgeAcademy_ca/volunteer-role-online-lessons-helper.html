Volunteer role

Online lessons helper

Do both halves of the hour: the messages around the lesson - it is happening, remember the time, we are starting - and the help inside it, answering students and parents in the chat while it runs.

What you do

  • Send the announcements and reminders to students and parents before the lesson.
  • Cover what they need to know: the lesson is happening, and the time to be there.
  • Join the live lesson with the teacher.
  • Answer student and parent questions in the chat while the lesson runs.
  • Follow up with anyone who missed it or left early.
  • Tell the teacher afterwards what you saw from your side.

What you need

  • Confident writing messages to students and parents.
  • Punctual with the reminders, and quick in the chat.
  • Comfortable in a live online session.
  • Clear in writing, and patient with repeat questions.
  • Reliable about the lessons you said you would cover.

How hours work

One or more lessons - you decide how many you manage

Each lesson you take runs for its own slot, plus the messages around it and the chat during it.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says what you supported
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample: we give you one lesson to prepare - the messages you would send around it, and how you would help a chat that fills up.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
