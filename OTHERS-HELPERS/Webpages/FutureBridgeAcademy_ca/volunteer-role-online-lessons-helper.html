Volunteer role

Online lessons helper

Sit inside the live lesson with the teacher: answer the questions in the chat, look after the students the teacher cannot reach in the moment, and keep the messages to students and parents moving.

  • One or more lessons
  • You choose how many
  • Online
Apply for this role All volunteer roles

What you do

  • Be in the live online lesson with the teacher.
  • Answer student questions in the chat while the lesson runs.
  • Check on the students who have gone quiet, so nobody is lost inside the lesson.
  • Message students and parents about dates, follow-up and anything they missed.
  • Tell the teacher afterwards what you saw from your side.

What you need

  • Comfortable in a live online session, with two screens or one open chat.
  • Quick to reply, and clear in writing.
  • Good at noticing the student who went quiet.
  • Reliable about the lessons you said you would cover.

Time required

One or more lessons - you decide how many you manage

Each lesson you take runs for its own slot, plus a few minutes of messages around it.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says what you supported
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample: we describe a lesson where three students are stuck at once and you tell us the order you would handle them in.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
