Why the programme exists

Families deserve better than a choice between free and good

There is plenty of free material online, and there is expensive tutoring. In between sits most families, especially those new to the Canadian school system.

  • 🌐
    A system that is hard to read

    Which grades shape which options, what a prerequisite actually blocks, how IB, AP, college and trades compare: parents learn it slowly, and usually after a decision has already passed.

  • ♻️
    Practice that misses the point

    Endless worksheets do not fix a gap. Work matched to the topic the class is on today does, because it lands at the moment it can still change the school term.

  • 🤝
    Support that disappears

    A student who is behind needs the same teacher, the same rhythm and someone who notices: that is what a steady volunteer mentor gives, and it costs nothing.

What we actually do

Three legs, and none of them stands alone

🔢

Academic support

Free weekly online lessons in mathematics, a free in-person mock exam every month, practice matched to the school topic, and mentors who correct a wrong turn in the moment.

🧭

Pathway guidance

Help with the decisions after high school - university, college, trades, IB and AP - including what each one asks for and when. Seminars run now; written pathway plans come with the centre.

🏠

A place where study happens

A centre with rooms, routines and supervision, so study has a home of its own rather than the end of a kitchen table. It opens as the programme grows.

Who is behind it

A programme, run with partners, under a charity

Future Bridge Academy is the programme's trading name. It is led by CentreCanada Newcomer Services Society, a registered charity in the Vancouver area, and delivered together with partner non-profit organisations and charities in other Canadian cities.

One programme, one set of standards, local partners who know their own community.

Where we are today

Free things first, the centre after

We would rather run something real for free than announce something impressive that does not exist yet.

Running today, free

  • Weekly online lessons in mathematics
  • A monthly mock exam, in person, on the school topic
  • Scholarship seminars and parent seminars
  • The community channel and our volunteer mentors
  • The drone pilot certificate course, taught online

Opens with the centre

  • Small-group lessons at the centre
  • One-to-one mentoring with a monthly growth report
  • Written pathway plans and application portfolio tracking
  • The CEMC contest club, as its own separate track
  • English and writing, taught the same way as maths

No opening date yet: we will not publish one until it is confirmed, because a date we cannot keep is worse than no date at all.

How we behave

Three promises you can hold us to

  • 💰
    No prices on this website

    We never publish prices, and when something carries an optional fee we explain it before you agree to anything.

  • ✅
    Nothing described as running unless it is

    Programmes that open with the centre are always labelled that way, and we never borrow someone else's results or make up numbers about ours.

  • 🔒
    Student information stays protected

    We only ask for what is needed to run the lessons, we handle it as Canadian privacy practice requires, and photos or quotes are never used without written permission.
