Open positions

Ten roles, and the time each one really takes

All ten positions are open now — no waiting lists. Most work is online, from any city in Canada.

01

Online maths teacher

1 hour a week · weekly lesson

Teach the weekly free maths lesson from our lesson plan, and explain it a second way when a student is stuck.

Good fit if: you know maths to grade 12 and can hold a fixed weekly slot.

02

Mock exam organiser

6–8 hrs per exam · max one every 2 months

Run the monthly exam end to end: registrations, exam-day help, marking and results for the families.

Good fit if: you are organised and free on the exam day you take.

03

Scholarship presenter (English)

Your pace · presenting hours agreed

Find the scholarships that are really open, build the shared slides and present them in English.

Good fit if: you read official sources and enjoy slides or presenting.

04

Scholarship presenter (other languages)

Your pace · presenting hours agreed

Translate the scholarship pages and present the seminars in your language for families who prefer it.

Good fit if: you are fluent in English and one other language.

05

Self-paced course assistant

WhatsApp group · your schedule

Guide a student group through a self-paced course and answer questions in the group as they move through subjects.

Good fit if: you reply quickly and notice who has fallen behind.

06

Curriculum builder

From 1 hour a week · your pace

Pick small, self-contained tasks from our long list and finish them one at a time — you always know what is done.

Good fit if: you know the subject and can carry on without being chased.

Open positions — continued

07

Online lessons helper

One or more lessons · you choose

Support the teacher in the live lesson: answer chat questions and message families about dates and follow-up.

Good fit if: you are comfortable live and spot the student who went quiet.

08

Academic writing guide

Hours agreed with programme lead

Set the subjects, give the writing tasks and coach structure and editing through the platform.

Good fit if: you write well and coach without rewriting the work.

09

Career coach

Hours agreed with programme lead

Guide one high-school student at a time — from setting the goal together to a complete, ready-to-use file.

Good fit if: you have walked a university, college or trades path.

10

Career path presenter (professionals only)

Hours agreed with programme lead

Present your profession honestly: the day-to-day, pay, required licences and what to prepare at school now.

Good fit if: you work in the profession today and take questions well.

How we select volunteers

Five steps, the same for every applicant

1 Apply

Position, strengths, availability, references.

2 Interview

A short video chat about your experience.

3 Teaching sample

Explain a topic — we ask the awkward questions.

4 References

We contact the people you named.

5 Decision

Accepted or declined — you always hear back.
