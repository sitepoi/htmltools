Where to start

Most families begin with one free lesson

Come to a free online lesson, or sit a free mock exam. Nothing is measured against anyone else and nothing is expected of you afterwards.

  • 🚀
    Start free, tonight

    The free lessons and the monthly mock exams are open to every family, whether or not you join anything else.

  • 💬
    Ask first, decide later

    Send one message with your child's grade and the topic they are on. We answer honestly, including when we are not the right fit.

  • 📅
    Add the rest when it opens

    Small groups, mentoring and pathway guidance arrive with the centre. Families in the community hear about each one first.
