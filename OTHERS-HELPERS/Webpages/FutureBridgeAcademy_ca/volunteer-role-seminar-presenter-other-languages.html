Volunteer role

Scholarship seminar presenter (other languages)

Take the scholarship research, the records in our system and the shared presentation, and deliver them in another language for families who are more comfortable outside English, so no deadline or document requirement gets lost in translation.

What you do

  • Research the scholarships that are open now or opening soon, from official sources only.
  • Add each one to our system as a record with every detail that matters: value, eligibility, required documents, deadlines, essay or reference requirements, and the official link.
  • Translate the parts that matter precisely: eligibility, documents, deadlines, money.
  • Build the pages of our shared presentation from those records in your language: one scholarship per page, in the order families have to act on them.
  • Present the seminar and answer questions parents are often too shy to ask in English.
  • Tell us which questions come up again, so we can answer them earlier next time.

Two parts, one role. The research and data work, the presenting work, or both - you agree with the programme lead which part you take. Some volunteers start with the records and move to presenting later, and some only ever prepare the data, which we value just as much.

What you need

  • Fluent in English and in the language you present in.
  • Comfortable presenting online: our seminars run as video calls, and they can reach hundreds of students and parents at once.
  • For the research part: careful with numbers, dates and translation, because they have to be exact in both languages.
  • You know the scholarship process, or you are willing to learn it properly first.

How hours work

Hours agreed with the programme lead

Research and translation are self paced. Presenting hours are agreed and you are asked before you are put on a seminar date.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says what you presented
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form, choose this role, and say which language and which part you want: research and data, presenting, or both.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample in your language: either one scholarship added as a complete record, or a ten minute seminar outline on one scholarship translated back into English for us.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
