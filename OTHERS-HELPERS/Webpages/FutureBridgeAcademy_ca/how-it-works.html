Every month, students sit a short exam in person at a community venue, on the topic they are working on. It measures personal progress - never a ranking against the class. Contest maths runs as a completely separate track for those who want it.

  • Free monthly mock exam, held in person
  • Results feed straight back into the plan
  • Optional CEMC contest club (Gauss to Euclid), kept separate

Questions parents ask

Before you join, the honest answers

Are the online lessons really free?

Yes. The weekly online lessons, the monthly mock exams, the scholarship seminars and the parent seminars are free, and they have been running that way. Optional programmes that come later - small-group lessons, one-to-one mentoring, the drone course - are the ones that may carry a fee, and we tell you clearly before you commit to anything.

How do we start?

Come to a free online lesson, or to a monthly mock exam, and see how it feels. When you are ready to go further we do a short assessment and a goal conversation - and you keep the written summary either way.

Online, in centre, or both?

Online today: the weekly lessons and the seminars. In person today: the monthly mock exams at community venues. Small-group lessons and one-to-one mentoring open as the centre does.

How large are the groups?

Small, deliberately. The free online lessons are open to everyone in our community, and when the small-group lessons open we keep the groups small enough that every learner gets attention - that is the whole point of having a room.

What if my child is ahead of the class, or behind?

The plan starts where your child actually is. The level sets the pace, so being ahead means more depth rather than repetition, and being behind means smaller steps, not less support.

How will I know it is working?

Every month: a mock exam on the topic your child is studying, plus a growth report covering topics, work habits and the next goal. If something is not working, you will see it as early as we do.

Do you do contests and competitions?

Contest maths (CEMC - Gauss, Pascal, Cayley, Fermat, Euclid) runs as its own track for students who want it, and it never replaces the core programme. The monthly mock exams are a separate thing entirely, and they are free for everyone.

Can we pause, or change subject?

Yes. Math comes first and English follows the same model, so changing subject is a plan update rather than a fresh start - and a pause keeps everything on file for when you come back.
