Volunteer role

Academic writing guide

Set the writing subjects, give the tasks to the students, and follow how each one is doing so the feedback reaches the piece they are actually writing.

What you do

  • Set the subjects and the writing tasks for each student.
  • Write the task clearly enough that the student knows what a finished piece looks like.
  • Follow how each student is moving through the tasks in our system.
  • Give feedback on structure, argument and editing, not on the student's opinions.
  • Flag the students who need a real conversation instead of another comment.

What you need

  • You write well yourself and can explain why a sentence works.
  • You coach the student's writing instead of rewriting it for them.
  • Comfortable working in a system rather than by email attachments.
  • Consistent: feedback that arrives late teaches nothing.

How hours work

Hours agreed with the programme lead

Set by how many students you take, and agreed with you before you start.

What you get

  • Training for the role, before you start
  • Your hours in writing, whenever you need them
  • A reference that says what you guided
  • A letter of support on request
  • A review at the end of your term

How to apply

  1. Send the volunteer form and choose this role.
  2. Short interview, then a work sample: we give you a short student piece and you send back the feedback you would give, in the tone you would use.
  3. We contact the two references you named, then tell you the decision either way.

Training is provided for this role before you start. Roles with a heavier training load ask for a longer commitment, and we tell you what that is before you agree to it.
