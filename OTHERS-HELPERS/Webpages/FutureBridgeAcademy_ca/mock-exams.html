How it works

Topic-based testing, not a year in a single paper

Each exam is built around the section your child is on at school, so it measures whether the teaching landed instead of how much can be memorised in one sitting.

01

We test the topic you are on

Tell us the section the class is studying and the paper is built from that, so nothing is asked about material the students have not met yet.

02

You sit it under exam conditions

In person, at a community venue, with a clock running and no phone on the desk. That is what teaches exam calm, and it cannot be practised at the kitchen table.

03

It is marked and explained

Students get the mark, but the useful part is the explanation: which steps were missing, and what to do differently next time.

04

It sets the next month

The results decide what we practise in the following weeks, so the exam is a planning tool rather than a verdict.

Why it is free

Because the community runs it

Families should not have to pay to find out where their child stands. The exams are written and marked by volunteers, and the community makes the room available, so nothing is passed on to parents.

  • 🤝

  • 🏠
    Hosted by the community

    Community venues give us the space, which is why we can keep the exam at no cost to families.

  • 🎯
    Open to every family

Good to know

Every mock exam is held in person

There is no online version of the monthly mock exam. Sitting in a room with a clock and a paper is the whole point, so we keep it that way.

If you cannot make the next date, tell us. The exam runs again the following month.

Two different things

The monthly exam and contest maths never mix

The monthly mock exam follows the topic your child is studying at school. Contest preparation for Gauss, Pascal, Cayley, Fermat and Euclid is a separate track that opens with the centre, for students who actively want it. One never stands in for the other, and passing one tells you nothing about the other.

  • Monthly mock exam: school topic, everyone welcome, free, in person
  • Contest track: contest maths, by choice, a deliberately separate programme

Questions parents ask

The honest answers

Is the mock exam really free?

Yes. The monthly mock exam costs families nothing. Volunteers write, invigilate and mark it, and the community provides the venue. We never publish prices, and where something does carry an optional fee we say so before you agree to anything.

Can my child sit it online?

No. Every mock exam is held in person at a community venue. There is no online version, because the value is in the room, the clock and the paper.

Which topics will be tested?

The section your child is studying at school when the exam runs. You tell us the topic when you register and the paper is built around it.

Does my child need to prepare?

No cramming and no revision book. The exam is designed to show what to work on next, so an imperfect result is useful information rather than a failure.

Is this the same as contest preparation?

No, and we keep them deliberately separate. The monthly mock exam follows the school topic and is for everyone. Contest preparation for CEMC competitions is its own track for students who want it.
