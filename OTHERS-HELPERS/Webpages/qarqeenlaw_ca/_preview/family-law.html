Common questions

Quick answers

How do family law matters usually proceed?

Most start with a careful review of your situation, then clear advice on your options - whether that leads to agreement, negotiation or court.

What about the children?

Child custody and support are central in many family matters. We focus on arrangements that work for the children while protecting your interests.

Do I have to go to court?

Not always. We explain which matters can be resolved by agreement and when court is needed.
