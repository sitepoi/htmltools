Common questions

Quick answers

Why do I need a will?

A will makes sure your wishes are honoured and your loved ones are protected, and it avoids unnecessary delay and cost.

What is included in estate planning?

Drafting wills, establishing trusts where they help, and managing estate administration.

How often should a will be reviewed?

Review your will when your circumstances change - marriage, children, a move, or a major change in your assets.
