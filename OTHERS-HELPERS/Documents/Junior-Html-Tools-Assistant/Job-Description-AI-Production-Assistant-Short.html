AI Production Assistant
30 hours per week

We need one person who works well with AI and turns a request into a finished, checked result. The work covers three things: testing our applications, building small applications, and creating content. We train you on the method, the templates and the checks.

What you will do

  • Test applications and tools. Follow the test list, try to break things, and report what is wrong with clear steps and a screenshot.
  • Build small applications. Create single-purpose tools with AI inside our template, then test them like a real user.
  • Create content. Website content, education content or other content. Research the source, structure it, write it, and check that it is complete and correct.
  • Report honestly. What passed, what failed, what you are unsure about, and what needs a decision.

What we need from you

  • Comfortable reading and writing English.
  • Comfortable with a computer and an AI assistant.
  • Follows written steps exactly, and is careful with names, numbers and dates.
  • Says clearly when something fails or is unclear.

No previous software experience is required.
