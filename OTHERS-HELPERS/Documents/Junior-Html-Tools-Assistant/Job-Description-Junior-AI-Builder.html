Junior AI Builder
Curriculum and Tools - 10-week role with full training
Document: JD-2026-07 | Version: 1.0 | Date: 2026-10-28

We are looking for one junior person to build two things with AI: short courses and small web tools. You do not need to be a programmer or a teacher. We will train you from the first day, and you will work on real material that we review together.

This is a paid, remote, 10-week role. Your work will be used by real people, so care and honesty matter more than speed.

1. What you will do

Build courses

  • Research an official guide or handbook for a subject.
  • Turn it into a clear outline: sections and lessons.
  • Prepare a short PDF for each lesson, taken from the official source.
  • Use our Curriculum Builder to generate lesson text, slides, flashcards and quizzes.
  • Read the result like a student and fix what is confusing.

Build small tools

  • Write a one-page plan for a simple tool before building it.
  • Build it with AI inside our template, in small steps.
  • Test it yourself: empty fields, long text, wrong numbers, reload, phone screen.
  • Fix what fails and test again.
  • Hand it over with a short note of what you tested.

Check and improve

  • Use our existing tools and courses like a first-time user, and write down what is broken.
  • Give steps that anyone can repeat to see the same problem.
  • Keep clear notes, so another person can continue your work.

2. What we will teach you

All training is paid and happens during working hours. No prior experience is needed.

When What you learn
Week 1 How our files and folders are organised, our work rules, the tool template, Curriculum Builder, and how to prepare a source PDF from an official guide.
Week 2 How to write a one-page tool plan, how to build with AI in small steps, and how to run a test checklist.
Week 3 onwards One new skill each week with written feedback: research from sources, writing for beginners, writing quiz questions, accessibility, and checking your own work.

3. What we expect from you

Required

  • You can read and write English clearly.
  • You follow written steps exactly, including file names.
  • You are comfortable using AI tools and willing to check their answers.
  • You say clearly when something fails or when you are unsure.
  • You ask for help within 30 minutes of being stuck.
  • You can work [30 hours per week] for 10 weeks.

Nice to have, not required

  • Turkish, Spanish or another language.
  • You can read simple HTML and CSS.
  • You have tutored, taught or explained something to someone.
  • You have used ChatGPT, Claude or similar every day.

Not required: a university degree, programming experience, a teaching certificate, or a portfolio.

4. How we work

  • Short daily update. Three written lines: what you did, what is blocking you, what is next.
  • Monday plan, Friday review. We agree the week on Monday and review it together on Friday.
  • Proof with every delivery. A file path, a screenshot or a test result. "It works" is not enough.
  • Written feedback. You always get a clear reason when something is accepted or returned.
  • You never publish. We review and publish after you hand over.

Five rules that never change

  • Never use git, the database, or publishing tools. Those stay with us.
  • Work only on the files and tasks you are given.
  • Never invent a fact, a price, a law, a rule or a source page.
  • Never copy text or code without checking where it came from.
  • When in doubt, ask first and wait.

5. What success looks like after 10 weeks

  • You can build a small tool from a one-page plan without help.
  • You can turn an official guide into a course where every lesson is covered and checked.
  • Most of your work passes review the first time.
  • Another person can pick up your notes and continue.
  • You can explain what you built, and what you tested, in plain words.
