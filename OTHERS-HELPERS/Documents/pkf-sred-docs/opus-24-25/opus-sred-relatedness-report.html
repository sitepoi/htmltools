1. Purpose and Method

This report compares the OpusChart SR&ED technical narrative (T661 lines 240, 242, 244, and 246) against the Resource Management task list in ClickUp and scores how closely the narrative reflects the actual development work recorded in the task management system.

Method: every one of the 217 exported tasks was reviewed. Each claim made by the narrative (uncertainty, work performed, advancement) was matched against supporting tasks by keyword and by manual reading of task titles. Supporting task IDs are cited in Section 4. The score combines theme-level evidence with the quality of the task records as proof of a systematic investigation.

This is version 2.2 of the report. The task source was replaced: the earlier chart-board export was the wrong list, and this report now uses the Resource Management board (opus_chart_rm_sprint_10_to_32.json, Sprints 10 to 32). All scores were recalculated, the fiscal year (1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025) was confirmed, and completion of all "ready for deployment" tasks was confirmed, which brings every task in the export inside the claim year and resolves the status ambiguity. Task IDs belong to the Resource Management board; the same numbers on other boards refer to different tasks.

7. Risks If the CRA Reviews These Records

  1. AI section is unfinished. The AI tasks are in-year but are research and in-progress development with no completed result to claim as an advancement.
  2. Bug-flavoured titles. "Bug on calculating hours in budget tab" (169), "Budget bug" (34), and "Hourly rate tab gives error" (54) read as routine bug fixing, which is excluded work.
  3. Task ID overlap between boards. The same ID numbers appear on the other ClickUp boards. Any cited task must name the Resource Management board to avoid confusion in a review.
  4. No dates in the narrative. The task log spans July 2024 to April 2025. The narrative gives no period, which weakens the tax-year scoping.

8. Recommendations to Raise the Score

  1. Keep the AI section as an investigation, not an advancement. The fiscal year (ending 30 June 2025) includes the AI tasks, so describe the vector database and RAG research (tasks 1242, 1243) honestly and state that no recommendation capability was completed.
  2. Add dates and Resource-Management task IDs to line 244. Cite the IDs from Section 4 and name the board, since IDs overlap with other ClickUp lists.
  3. Reframe calculation bugs as experimental observations. Tasks 169, 34, 42, 49, 50, and 73 describe model iterations. Present them as observed inconsistencies from tested calculation models, not as bug fixes.
  4. Keep "profitability" out of the claim. The only evidence is task 37, a data field. Use "project cost, budget consumption, and remaining balances" wording.
  5. Use the named staff and tracked hours for T661 Part 6. Four developers plus product support are named, with substantial tracked time (for example, 45h 54m on task 367 and 73h 5m on task 159).
