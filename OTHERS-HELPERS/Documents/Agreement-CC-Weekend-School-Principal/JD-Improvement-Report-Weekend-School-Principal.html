1. Purpose of This Report

Two source documents for the Weekend School Principal role were combined into one English Job Description:

  • Source A: the Turkish JD-2026-04 (Görev Tanımı - Hafta Sonu Okulu Müdürü), a formal agreement-style job description.
  • Source B: the Horizon Weekend School School Principal Job Description (PDF), a detailed operating guide with duties, rules, schedule and pay tiers.

The combined document is Job-Description-Weekend-School-Principal.html in this folder. This report lists the conflicts found between the two sources, recommends further improvements, proposes HTML tools to support the school operations, and ends with a task list to finalize the document.

6. HTML Tools to Support Operations

6.1 Student-Based Application (to develop later)

One student-centered application where each student record holds all the modules below. The modules are sections of the same app, not separate apps. More modules can be added to the student record later as needs appear.

Module Supports (JD section) What it holds per student
a. Registration and Parent Information 4B, 4D, 4G Student registration record, parent contact details, emergency contacts, allergies and health notes, pick-up authorization, photo consent.
b. Payments - Monthly Tracker 4I, 5 (pay brackets) Monthly payment status per student, polite late reminders, monthly payment list, unique-student count for the pay bracket.
c. Student Progress Tracker (general) 4C.5, 4B.3 General progress notes, weekly teacher contact log, flag for dropping attendance / interest.
d. Student Quran Level Tracker 4C.5 Current Quran reading level per student and its history.
e. Student Quran Memorization Level Tracker 4C.5 Hifz progress per student: surahs and portions memorized, next targets.
f. Parent Communication Log 4A, 9 Weekly group message archive (English and Turkish), one-on-one feedback, same-day absent-student call log, suggestions and complaints follow-up.
g. Weekly and Monthly Reports Generator 4J, 9, 10 Aggregates attendance, payments, progress highlights and issues per student; produces the WhatsApp export and the report for the monthly in-person meeting.
h. Attendance - Weekly, Lesson-Based 4C.1 - 4C.3 Attendance per lesson for each Sunday, completed within 24 hours, missing-entry reminders.

Note: the JD requires everything to be recorded in ATLAS today (JD Section 4C and the Qualifications section). Until the student application is ready, ATLAS stays the source of record; the app can later export to or sync with ATLAS.

Optional future modules for the student record: term progress reports (report cards), awards and certificates per student, Quran level change history.
