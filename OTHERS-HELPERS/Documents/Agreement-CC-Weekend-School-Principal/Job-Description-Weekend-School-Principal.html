Weekend School Principal - Horizon Weekend School
(Hafta Sonu Okulu Müdürü)

1. Position Summary

The Weekend School Principal is responsible for the overall management of CentreCanada Newcomer Services Society's Horizon Weekend School: academic planning, teacher management, parent communication, student registration, fee collection, school events and the school's visibility in the community. The Principal is the face of the school and the first point of contact for parents.

  • Overall School Management - annual plan, class schedule, classroom arrangements, opening and closing calendar.
  • Teacher Management - hiring, orientation, class observations, support and substitute teacher planning.
  • Parent Communication - announcements, parent meetings, follow-up of suggestions and complaints.
  • Student and Registration Management - promotion, open days, registration process, class lists and attendance tracking.
  • Fees, Events and Records - collections, receipts, payment follow-up, school events and student files.
This job description forms part of the employment contract / agreement between the organization and the employee. It defines the scope of the role, the responsibilities and the expected performance standards. It may be updated from time to time by mutual written agreement.

2. About the School

Horizon Weekend School is a Sunday school for children from families of different cultures. Most students come from Turkish families, but students of other nationalities are also welcome.

Courses

  • Core courses: Quran, memorization (hifz), Islamic studies.
  • Elective and enrichment courses: Turkish, art and other activities.

Goals of the School

  1. Help children spend their weekend in a productive and meaningful way.
  2. Help children from different cultures meet, bond and build friendships.
  3. Make sure every student makes real progress in Quran, Islamic studies and memorization.
  4. Create a fun, warm environment where children love coming to school.

3. Core Responsibilities

A. Overall School Management

  • Take on the general management of the school: annual plan, class schedule, classroom arrangements, opening and closing calendar.

B. Teacher Management

  • Recruit, orient and manage teachers: class observations, feedback, support and substitute teacher planning.

C. Parent Communication

  • Communicate regularly with parents: announcements, parent meetings, follow-up of suggestions and complaints.

D. Student and Registration Management

  • Find students and manage registrations: promotion, open days, registration process, class lists and attendance tracking.

E. Fee and Payment Management

  • Run fee and payment management: collections, receipts, payment follow-up and financial reports presented to the Executive Director.

F. Events and School Records

  • Organize school events such as report cards, end-of-year shows, graduations, trips and special days.
  • Keep student files and school records organized and up to date.

G. Compliance and Internal Coordination

  • Run the school's registration, fee and event processes in line with organizational policies and legal requirements; follow child protection and privacy rules. School fees must include 5% GST, as the organization is registered for GST.
  • Coordinate school activities with the Education Coordinator according to the organization's annual plan.
  • Work in harmony with other departments such as finance and communications; make sure incoming e-mails and requests do not go unanswered.
  • Keep school-related correspondence, records and files organized and up to date.

4. Detailed Duties and Responsibilities

A. Parent Relations and Communication

  1. Show patience and understanding toward parents in every situation. Listen to their concerns, help and support them. When tension arises, stay calm and resolve the issue respectfully.
  2. Send a group message to all parents every week: which lessons were covered, what was done and what is coming next week. Messages are also sent in English for parents who do not speak Turkish.
  3. Contact parents one-on-one when needed to give feedback about their child.
  4. Contact the parent of any absent student on the same day, learn the reason and record it.
  5. Make sure the community sees what happens at the school, so the community feels ownership of it.
  6. Hold at least one parent meeting per term.

B. Student Registration and Follow-Up

  1. Register at least 26 students during the year. The annual target is 30 students.
  2. Set monthly enrollment goals to grow the number of students and share them with the Education Coordinator.
  3. Make sure every student is contacted at least once during the week by their teacher for guidance and coaching. Assign students to teachers and check that these calls happen.
  4. Identify students whose attendance or interest is dropping and take action.

C. Academic Tracking and ATLAS Records

  1. Make sure the Principal and all teachers take daily attendance in the ATLAS app.
  2. For every lesson, ATLAS entries include the topics covered and notes on each student (for example: "Today we worked on Surah Al-Fatiha. Ahmet completed his memorization; Zeynep will continue next week.").
  3. ATLAS entries must be completed within 24 hours. Everyone is strongly recommended to complete ATLAS at school, right after classes end, before leaving.
  4. Check teachers' entries every week and remind them of anything missing.
  5. Track each student's Quran level and memorization progress.
  6. Coordinate student progress reports at the end of each term.

D. Photos, Videos and Documentation

  1. Make sure photos and videos of lessons and activities are taken every week.
  2. Upload them every week, organized in weekly folders, to the Google Drive folder chosen by CentreCanada or to ATLAS (the destination will be confirmed later).
  3. Collect the photo consent form from parents once at the beginning of the year for website and social media use. Consent is not mandatory for parents, but will be requested.
  4. Only share images of children whose parents have given photo consent.

E. Teacher and Volunteer Coordination

  1. Prepare the class schedule. Identify students taking the Turkish elective and set up classes and level groups.
  2. Arrange a substitute when a teacher cannot come.
  3. Explain the school's rules and procedures to new teachers.
  4. Observe each teacher's class at least once per month and give constructive feedback.
  5. Recruit volunteers beyond the teaching staff as the school needs them (classroom help, events, supervision, photography, etc.).
  6. Give volunteers their tasks, explain the school's rules to them and follow up on their work.
  7. Record volunteer hours on ATLAS and coordinate with the Volunteer Coordinator for preparing and presenting the certificates.

F. Planning and Events

  1. Prepare the annual calendar together with the Education Coordinator.
  2. Run all programs according to the calendar.
  3. Lead Kandil, Eid and camp programs, and plan, organize and follow up on children's activities for these events.
  4. Plan and organize the end-of-year show and exhibition. Prepare the folk dance team, the choir group and similar groups for the show. Organize the certificate and award ceremony.
  5. Organize parent volunteers for events and assign their tasks.
  6. Make sure groups such as folk dance and choir practise regularly throughout the year and perform at events and Kandil/Eid programs.

G. Child Safety

  1. Make sure every student's emergency contacts, allergies and health notes are recorded and easy to access. Collect the emergency information form from parents once at the beginning of the year.
  2. Follow the morning drop-off and afternoon pick-up procedure. A child is released only to their parent or a person the parent has authorized.
  3. Enforce the two-adult rule: no staff member or volunteer is ever alone with a child in a closed room.
  4. Make sure children are supervised during lunch and breaks.
  5. Know what to do in an emergency and explain it to teachers.
  6. Make sure every teacher and every volunteer who works directly with children has a valid Criminal Record Check.

H. Building and Facilities

  1. Be responsible for protecting the building and the spaces used, and for leaving them clean and tidy.
  2. Identify missing items and repairs needed, report them to the Education Coordinator and follow up until they are fixed.
  3. Do the opening and closing checks (lights, doors, classrooms, materials).

I. Fee Follow-Up

  1. Track parents' payments (made to CentreCanada's accounting office or online).
  2. Politely remind parents whose payments are late.
  3. Share the payment status with the Education Coordinator every month and provide financial reports to the Executive Director as required.

J. Reporting and Meetings

  1. Attend regular weekly meetings with the Education Coordinator.
  2. Submit a short monthly report: number of students, attendance rate, payment status, highlights and issues.

6. Working Rules

  1. The Principal arrives before everyone else, opens the building and gets everything ready.
  2. The Principal and all teachers must be at school and ready at least 15 minutes before classes start.
  3. A teacher who cannot come must inform the Principal by Saturday evening at the latest, so the Principal can arrange a substitute.

7. Sample Sunday Schedule

9:45 a.m.Teachers are at school and ready; student drop-off begins
10:00 a.m. - 3:00 p.m.Classes, lunch break and activities (photos and videos taken)
3:00 p.m.Students are released to their parents

8. Qualifications and Skills

  • Education: Any educational training, experience or graduation.
  • Experience: Experience in school management, teaching or teacher coordination, and in working with children and families, is preferred.
  • Management: Ability to manage teacher teams, delegate tasks and follow up.
  • Communication: Excellent verbal and written communication skills with parents, students and organizations.
  • Organization: Ability to manage records, payments, events and calendars at the same time.
  • Legislation: Knowledge of education and child protection rules in BC, or the ability to learn them quickly.
  • Languages: Fluency in English; additional languages (Turkish, Arabic, Persian, etc.) are an asset.
  • Tools: Comfortable with digital tools such as WhatsApp and Google Drive; command of office software and e-mail is an asset. ATLAS is the school's custom record-keeping application - the Principal must be willing to learn it as soon as possible and record and follow everything there.
  • Personal Qualities: Patient, friendly, organized and responsible.
  • Checks: Valid Criminal Record Check (standard in BC for anyone working with children). First aid certificate preferred.

9. Monitoring and Reporting

This role is closely supervised by the Education Coordinator (Lumina Unit):

  • Weekly: Report everything on ATLAS; send an export or weekly report over WhatsApp to the Education Coordinator; review it together in an in-person weekly meeting; and send the weekly parent information message.
  • Monthly: Written report and in-person meeting presenting the reports collected in the ATLAS app: student numbers, registration status, attendance tracking, fee collection, parent feedback and the priorities for the next month.

The formats above were confirmed by the parties.

10. Success Indicators

  • Number of students during the year (at least 26, target 30).
  • Attendance rate.
  • Weekly parent messages sent without missing a week.
  • ATLAS entries completed within 24 hours.
  • Weekly photo and video archive kept up to date in the chosen location (Google Drive or ATLAS).
  • Calendar events held on time.
  • Parent satisfaction.

11. What the Principal Must Not Do

  1. Give fee discounts or exemptions, or spend money, without the Education Coordinator's approval.
  2. Use physical or verbal punishment, or humiliate a child in any way.
  3. Release a child to an unauthorized person.
  4. Argue with parents in group chats or on social media. Problems are solved one-on-one and calmly.
  5. Share information about students or families with third parties.
  6. Share photos or videos of children whose parents have not given consent.
  7. Add or remove programs from the calendar without approval.

12. Performance Expectations

  • Run the weekend school in an organized and professional manner; manage registration, collections and parent satisfaction.
  • Prepare and implement the annual plan, class schedule and event calendar on time.
  • Manage teachers effectively; maintain teaching quality through class observations and feedback.
  • Keep student files and school records organized and up to date.
  • Comply with legal, ethical and regulatory requirements in all activities.
  • Submit weekly and monthly reports on time and accurately.
  • Represent the organization's mission and values faithfully in all interactions.

In addition to the duties listed in this job description, other tasks may be assigned from time to time according to CentreCanada's needs.
