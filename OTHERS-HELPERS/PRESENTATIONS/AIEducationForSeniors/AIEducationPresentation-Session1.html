The one idea to remember

AI is the library friend

Imagine a friend who has read every book, article, and recipe in the world - in every language.

  • Extremely fast at summarizing, explaining, writing, and translating.
  • Never tired, never impatient, happy to repeat anything.
  • Does not think like a person - it guesses the next word, again and again, so well that it looks like thinking.

That friend is AI. You do not need to know how it works - just like you drive a car without knowing the engine.

Straight talk

What AI is not

It is not a person

No feelings, no opinions, no plans of its own. It answers what you ask - that is the whole job.

It does not know you

It only sees what you type in the chat. You decide what it ever learns about you.

It is not always right

It can state a mistake with full confidence - like some voices on the internet. Anything that matters gets checked.

So treat it like any source you meet online: take the helpful ideas, keep your own judgment. AI has no power over you - only the power you choose to give it.

The whole skill, on one slide

Ask well, and AI does the rest

A vague question gets a vague answer. A rich question gets a useful answer. The recipe has four parts:

Part 1

Who

Who are you, and who is this for?

"I am a 75-year-old grandmother..."

Part 2

What

What do you want, exactly?

"...I need a short birthday letter for my grandson..."

Part 3

Details

What matters to you?

"...he loves dinosaurs and football..."

Part 4

Format

How should the answer look?

"...about 60 words, warm and simple."

Remember it as: Who - What - Details - Format.

Tap to open - complete sample prompts
Sample 1 - a birthday letter Who: I am a 75-year-old grandmother. What: I need a short birthday letter for my grandson. Details: he loves dinosaurs and football. Format: about 60 words, warm and simple.
Sample 2 - a doctor visit Who: I am a 68-year-old patient. What: help me prepare for my appointment. Details: I take three medicines and my knees hurt. Format: a list of what to tell the doctor and five questions to ask.
Sample 3 - a week of dinners Who: I am cooking for one. What: plan five low-salt dinners. Details: easy, under eight ingredients each. Format: a table plus one shopping list.
Tap to open - make it better by talking

The first answer is a first draft. Improve it by talking - one spoken change at a time:

  • "Read it back to me" - you hear the draft.
  • "Warmer", "shorter", "remove the second paragraph" - one change per round.
  • "Give me two versions - one formal, one warm" - then pick.
  • "What would make this better? Three ideas" - the AI becomes your editor.
The loop in one line Draft - listen - say one change - listen again. Repeat until it sounds like you.

Hands-on 2 - Write it in your own language first

Prepare the document in your own language. Make it mature there. Only then translate it to English.

Step 1 - write it in your language [Your language:] My washing machine is broken and the store does not answer. Write a complaint letter to the store for me.
Adım 1 (Türkçe) Çamaşır makinem bozuldu ve mağaza dönmüyor. Benim için mağazaya bir şikayet mektubu yaz.
Step 2 - make it mature in your language [Your language:] Improve this letter. Make it polite but firm, under 200 words, with a place for my name and address.
Adım 2 (Türkçe) Bu mektubu iyileştir. Kibar ama kararlı olsun, 200 kelimenin altında, adım ve adresim için yer bırak.
Step 3 - translate to English Now translate this letter into English. Keep the tone natural and polite, like a real person wrote it.
Adım 3 (Türkçe) Şimdi bu mektubu İngilizceye çevir. Ton doğal ve nazik olsun, gerçek bir insan yazmış gibi.
Hands-on 3 - Is my English good enough? The translate-back check

Your English letter is ready - but is it correct? Let a second AI check the first one:

The check prompt Translate this back into [your language]: [paste the English letter]
Kontrol sorusu (Türkçe) Bunu [dilinize] geri çevirin: [İngilizce mektubu yapıştırın]

Now compare: does the translation back say the same as your original? If the meaning matches, your English is ready to send. If something changed, fix that part and check again. Use a different app for the check - if ChatGPT wrote the letter, use Claude or Copilot.

Türkçe: Karşılaştırın: geri çeviri orijinalinizle aynı anlama geliyor mu? Anlam uyuyorsa İngilizceniz göndermeye hazır demektir. Bir şey değiştiyse o kısmı düzeltin ve tekrar kontrol edin. Mektubu ChatGPT yazdıysa, kontrolü Claude ya da Copilot ile yapın.

Beyond the basics - small tricks, big time savings

Tricks that save you time

Be my translator

Tap the voice button: "Be my translator between English and Turkish." Two people, one phone.

Read it back to me

Any answer becomes spoken words - letters, menus, medicine labels, news.

Interview me

"Interview me one question at a time" - the AI writes your letter or story by asking you.

Two versions

"Give me two versions - one formal, one warm" - then keep the one you like.

What am I forgetting?

"I am travelling to Istanbul for five days - what am I forgetting?" - instant checklist.

Practice the call

"Let's practice a difficult phone call - you play the insurance agent, I will call you."

Tap to open - six more tricks

Remember me

"Remember that I like short answers and always answer me in Turkish."

Check itself

"Check this letter for mistakes, then translate it back to Turkish so I can verify."

The polite editor

"What would make this better? Three ideas - but do only the ones I choose."

Turn it into a table

"Put that into a simple table." - prices, schedules, medicines become easy to read.

Explain like I am ten

"Explain this like I am ten years old." - jargon disappears.

Make a quiz

"Make a five-question quiz about birds for my grandson."

Every trick works by voice - tap the microphone and talk. And you can take a photo of anything and ask: "What is this?"
