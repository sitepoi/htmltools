HOW TO USE THIS FILE
- In Kahoot, create ONE new kahoot and add each question below.
- Each question has: the question text, options A-D, the correct
  answer, and a short "Why" line you can say aloud after the reveal.
- Suggested settings: points ON, 20 second time limit, single correct answer.
- You can also copy the questions into Kahoot's spreadsheet import
  template (columns: Question, Answer 1-4, Correct answer, Time limit).

QUESTIONS 1-6 COME FROM SESSION 1 - "YOUR FRIENDLY EVERYDAY HELPER"
QUESTIONS 7-12 COME FROM SESSION 2 - "VOICE, BIG PROJECTS, AND SAFETY"

QUESTION 1  (20 seconds)
What do we call the question you send to AI?

A) A reply
B) A prompt
C) A password
D) A profile

CORRECT: B
WHY: Everything starts with a prompt. That is the word for the question
or instruction you give the AI.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 2  (20 seconds)
The four-part recipe for a good question is: Who, What, Details, and...

A) Format
B) Speed
C) Price
D) Grammar

CORRECT: A
WHY: Who - What - Details - Format. The format says how long and how
simple the answer should be.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 3  (20 seconds)
Which question will give the BEST letter from AI?

A) "letter"
B) "write letter landlord"
C) "I am a 74-year-old tenant. My tap has dripped for two weeks. Write a
   short polite letter to my landlord asking for a repair, under 150 words."
D) "help"

CORRECT: C
WHY: The full question has who, what, details, and format. The details
are the difference.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 4  (20 seconds)
The answer is too long. What do you type?

A) "make it longer please"
B) "make it shorter"
C) "stop talking"
D) "never mind"

CORRECT: B
WHY: "Make it shorter" is one of the five fix-up words: shorter, simpler,
step by step, with an example, another version.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 5  (20 seconds)
Can AI make mistakes?

A) Never
B) Sometimes - and with full confidence
C) Only on Mondays
D) Only with math

CORRECT: B
WHY: AI can be wrong and sound completely sure. Always check important
facts with official sources.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 6  (20 seconds)
Which of these should you NEVER type into a chat?

A) A question about birds
B) Your bank card number
C) A request for a recipe
D) A thank-you note

CORRECT: B
WHY: Golden rule one: no secrets in the chat - never passwords, card
numbers, or ID numbers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 7  (20 seconds)
Which assistant listens to your voice on an iPhone?

A) Siri
B) Merlin
C) Canva
D) Perplexity

CORRECT: A
WHY: Siri is built into the iPhone. "Hey Siri, remind me at 8 in the
evening" - no typing needed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 8  (20 seconds)
Which tool names a bird just by hearing its song?

A) Suno
B) Merlin Bird ID
C) CapCut
D) DeepL

CORRECT: B
WHY: Merlin Bird ID listens to birdsong and tells you the bird - free
and it feels like magic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 9  (20 seconds)
What is the FIRST step to write your book?

A) Print it immediately
B) Let AI interview you, one question at a time
C) Buy a publishing company
D) Design the cover first

CORRECT: B
WHY: The interview turns your answers into a chapter outline - the first
real piece of the book.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 10  (20 seconds)
You want answers with sources you can check. Which tool?

A) Perplexity
B) CapCut
C) Alexa
D) Suno

CORRECT: A
WHY: Perplexity shows a citation for every claim, so you can verify
before you trust - golden rule four.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 11  (20 seconds)
A caller sounds EXACTLY like your grandson and asks for urgent money.
What do you do?

A) Send the money fast
B) Hang up and call his real number
C) Give your card number to speed it up
D) Keep talking to be polite

CORRECT: B
WHY: Voices can be copied with AI. Hang up, call the real number, and
use your family code word.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTION 12  (20 seconds)
Which golden safety rule is REAL?

A) AI calls you first about your account
B) Share your password with AI so it helps faster
C) Never send money because of a phone call or message
D) If they sound urgent, it must be real

CORRECT: C
WHY: Golden rule two. Real organizations never demand gift cards or
urgent transfers. Urgency is the scammer's tool.
