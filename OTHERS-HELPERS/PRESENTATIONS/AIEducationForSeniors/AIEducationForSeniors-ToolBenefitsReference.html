AI tools and their benefits

A friendly guide to the tools shown in today's class - what each one does and the benefit it gives you. Keep this sheet beside your phone or computer and use it whenever you need help.

How to use this guide
  • Looking for help with something? Find the matching group below and read the Benefit for you column.
  • Already use a tool? You will find one more thing the same tool can do for you.
  • Not sure where to start? Try ChatGPT or DeepSeek first - the best all-rounders, and both free.
  • A tool is not on this list? Write it down and ask the instructor during the break.

1Chat and voice tools

ChatGPT (free plan)

All-rounder: letters, explanations, meal plans, photos, and the best live spoken translation by voice.

One app covers most daily needs - the recommended first tool.

DeepSeek (free plan)

Full chat and voice with the most generous free limits.

The best free choice for daily heavy use - our suggestion for the seminar.

Claude (free plan)

Long documents, warm careful writing, patient interviews.

Best for long letters, life stories, and polite personal writing.

Gemini (free plan)

Connected to Google: search, maps, YouTube, Gmail.

Great for travel plans and questions about places.

Microsoft Copilot (free plan)

Built into Windows, Word, and Outlook. Spoken translation backup.

Easiest for Windows users; the second voice translator.

Perplexity (free plan)

Answers with a source shown for every claim.

Verifying news and health claims without blind trust.

Meta AI (free)

Lives inside WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger.

No new app to learn - ask where you already chat.

2Translation and language

DeepL (free)

The most natural written translation of letters.

Letters keep a warm, human tone in both directions.

Google Translate / Apple Translate (free)

Written and camera translation; Conversation mode as backup.

Point the camera at signs, menus, and medicine boxes.

Google Lens (free)

Reads and translates anything printed through the camera.

Instant reading of labels, menus, and letters.

ChatGPT Voice (free)

Live two-way spoken translation - the best choice.

Natural conversation in two languages on one phone.

Duolingo (free)

Daily language practice games.

Learning a family language in small steps.

3Health and healthy aging

ChatGPT or Claude (free)

Meal and movement plans within your doctor's limits.

Independence in daily choices, no guesswork.

Medisafe (free)

Pill reminders and daily medication schedules.

Right medicine at the right time, no forgotten doses.

MyTherapy (free)

Pill reminders, dose records, and health reports to share with your doctor.

Right medicine at the right time, plus a report for the doctor.

Apple Health / Google Fit (built in)

Automatic step and activity counting.

Seeing your progress keeps you moving.

Be My Eyes (free)

Reads labels and surroundings aloud for low vision.

A helper in your pocket for daily reading.

Seeing AI (free)

Reads any text aloud through the camera.

Letters and papers become spoken words.

Phone live captions (built in)

Turns conversations into text on the screen.

Full participation despite hearing difficulty.

MedlinePlus / WHO (free)

Official health information and fact checks.

The only authorities that settle health claims.

Doctor visit helper + AI voice translator (free)

Questions before, translation during, summary after a visit.

Calmer visits where nothing important is forgotten.

4Photos and creativity

Google Photos Magic Editor / iPhone Clean Up (built in)

One-tap repair of old photos.

Family memories look their best.

MyHeritage photo tools (free tier)

Adds realistic color to old portraits.

Black-and-white family photos come alive.

DALL-E inside ChatGPT (free tier)

Creates an image from a written description.

Pictures for cards, slides, and fun.

Microsoft Designer (free)

Designs from one sentence.

Invitations and posters in minutes.

Adobe Firefly (free tier)

Image creation that is safe for selling.

Art you may sell without legal worry.

Canva Magic (free tier)

Flyers, posters, invitations, and presentations from a sentence.

Full designs for causes and events without design skills.

5Video, audio, and music

CapCut (free)

Easy video editing with automatic captions.

Family and hobby videos look professional.

Descript (free tier)

Edits audio by editing text.

Fix a recording by deleting sentences.

Otter.ai (free tier)

Turns voice recordings into text.

Memories and interviews become written keepsakes.

Suno (free tier)

Sings songs from your own lyrics. Class samples: sample 1, sample 2.

A personal song for birthdays and weddings.

Spotify AI DJ (with free account)

Finds music you will like.

New music without searching.

6Learning and hobbies

Khanmigo (Khan Academy)

A tutor built for students.

Grandchildren get guided homework help.

Merlin Bird ID (free)

Names birds by song or photo.

The garden and the park get names.

iNaturalist (free)

Names plants, mushrooms, and insects.

Walking in nature becomes a hobby.

7Home, money, and projects

Siri / Google Assistant / Alexa (built in)

Reminders, timers, calls, music by voice.

Hands-free help at home.

Snug (free version)

One tap a day to check in; family is alerted if a check-in is missed.

Peace of mind when living alone.

Grammarly (free tier)

Grammar and clarity pass on anything you wrote.

Letters leave with clean spelling.

Wix AI / Squarespace (free trials)

Builds a website from a description.

A shop or club page without code.

Amazon KDP / Blurb (free to publish)

Self-publishing a book.

Your life story becomes a real book.

Senior Helper AI Coordinator (free)

Big-button app: one tap per task, reads answers aloud.

The easiest entry point after the seminar.

8Brain and mind

MindMate (free)

Brain games, memory exercises, and mental health support built for seniors.

One app designed for older adults - a daily mental workout.

Lumosity (free tier)

Games for memory, attention, and problem solving.

A few free games a day keep the mind sharp.

Peak (free version)

Short daily workouts for memory and mental agility.

A personal training plan in a few minutes a day.

CogniFit (free tier)

Science-based games and cognitive assessments.

Tracks memory and focus over time.

Fit Brains Trainer (free tier)

Games for memory, speed, and logic by Rosetta Stone.

Short sessions, a different skill each day.

Happy Neuron (free tier)

Games for memory, attention, language, and reasoning.

Puzzles that feel like play.

NeuroNation (free tier)

Personalized training for memory and concentration.

Exercises tailored to you.

Brainwell (free)

Games for memory, problem solving, and critical thinking.

Daily challenges with levels.

Elevate (free version)

Games for comprehension, focus, and processing speed.

Personalized daily workouts.
