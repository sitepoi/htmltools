AI tools and their benefits

Presenter quick reference. Print this before the seminar and keep it beside you. Every tool covered today is here, with what it does and the benefit it gives - so you can answer questions from this sheet instead of searching or asking an AI live in front of the room.

How to answer questions with this sheet
  • Someone asks for a tool for a specific need - point to the matching group and read the benefit line.
  • Someone already uses a tool - welcome it, ask what they use it for, and show one more thing the same tool can do.
  • Someone names a tool that is not on this list - write it down and answer at the break. Never stop the seminar to search.

9. Questions about the future of AI

If someone asks: where is all this going?
  • Near future - 1 to 3 years: AI built into every phone and computer, better voice chats, forms and translations finished in seconds, doctors and banks using AI assistants - with a human always signing off. Meaning: fewer apps to learn, you will mostly talk to your phone.
  • Longer term - 5 to 15 years: personal assistants that remember appointments and medicines, health sensors watched over by the doctor, smarter homes and transport. Meaning: AI removes busywork - people stay in charge of money, health, and family decisions.
  • The one promise: the skill taught today - asking well - never goes out of date. And the five golden rules still work in 2035.

10. If someone asks: how do I make AI answers better?

The talk-and-improve loop
  • Draft - listen - say one change - listen again. Do it all by voice: "read it back to me", then "warmer", "shorter", "remove the second paragraph".
  • One change per round beats five at once.
  • Ask for options: "give me two versions - formal and warm".
  • Let AI suggest: "what would make this better? three ideas" - the learner decides which to keep.
  • For documents: draft, add, reorder, shorten, check facts, translate, read aloud - each step is one spoken instruction.
  • For the live demo, print the prompt palette - AIEducationForSeniors-LiveWritingDemo.html - universal follow-ups chosen by looking at the draft, never by guessing.
