SMALL BUSINESS PLAN - VANCOUVER CORRIDORS
Small Business Plan - BC Pilot

Vancouver Corridor Cargo Sharing
Kelowna, Kamloops and Victoria

A cost-sharing cargo marketplace on three BC corridors, run by a BC company, intra-provincial only. Drivers already traveling sell spare trunk space; senders contribute to trip costs instead of paying courier prices. No fleet, no employees, no operating authority required.

1The Three Corridors

CorridorRouteRoad distanceKey featurePilot role
Vancouver - KelownaHwy 1 / Coquihalla / Hwy 97C~390 kmLargest destination market; no ferry; steady student, family and business trafficLAUNCH FIRST
Vancouver - KamloopsHwy 1 / Coquihalla~355 kmSame highway spine as Kelowna - one driver pool serves bothLAUNCH SECOND
Vancouver - VictoriaHwy 17 + BC Ferries (Tsawwassen - Swartz Bay)~100 km + 1 h 35 ferryHighest travel volume, but ferry schedule and terminal handover add frictionLAUNCH THIRD

Why this order

Kelowna and Kamloops share the Coquihalla corridor, so one driver recruitment effort feeds two lanes and the second lane launches nearly free. Victoria is bigger but ferry-dependent: handovers must happen at terminals or on the Peninsula, which adds a first/last-mile problem - launch it once the playbook is proven.

Read this honestly

Three corridors will not fund a company on their own - Year 3 platform revenue is roughly one part-time salary. The pilot exists to prove retention, trust and repeatability; the business case is the corridor playbook applied to Ontario, Alberta and the rest of BC.

3Target Customers

Vancouver - Kelowna

Okanagan second-home owners, UBC/UBCO students, wineries and food producers, e-commerce sellers, trades sending parts, families sending boxes both ways. Longest lane, highest contribution per parcel.

Vancouver - Kamloops

Thompson Rivers University students, healthcare and public-sector staff, mining and forestry suppliers, online sellers in the Interior. Served by the same Coquihalla drivers as Kelowna.

Vancouver - Victoria

Government and professional services documents, university students, Island residents buying from Vancouver sellers, film and tech production materials. Ferry is the constraint - target senders whose parcels fit one-day handover windows.

4Pricing - Cost-Sharing Contribution

Published formula, no negotiation: contribution = distance band base + size band adder, capped so the parcel never pays more than a fair share of actual trip costs. Hard cap 60 kg.

Comparable express courier pricing on these lanes is typically $40-120 for small parcels; the contribution sits at roughly half to two thirds. Victoria is priced lower because the ferry crossing is a fixed driver cost the parcel does not add to.

6Launch Sequence and 90 Days

Phase 0 - Weeks 1-4: BC compliance and corridor setup

GOAL: legally clean, insurer signed
  • Business registration, GST, ICBC business-use onboarding step, municipal licences for Vancouver and Kelowna.
  • Platform master policy (cargo + non-owned auto) with one insurer; sender add-on product.
  • Build the Kelowna corridor page, pricing table and driver onboarding flow.

Phase 1 - Weeks 5-8: Kelowna pilot

GOAL: 40 verified drivers, 8 parcels per day
  • Recruit UBCO students, Okanagan commuters and second-home owners at campuses and Kelowna community groups.
  • Weekly proof: escrow delivery, QR handover, dispute-free trips.

Phase 2 - Weeks 9-12: Kamloops on the same spine

GOAL: same driver pool adds the second lane
  • Open Kamloops with the same pricing formula and handover points.
  • Target TRU students and Interior suppliers.

Phase 3 - Months 4-6: Victoria

GOAL: ferry-terminal handover solved
  • Handover points at Tsawwassen and Swartz Bay, plus downtown Vancouver and Victoria pickup hubs.
  • Launch only if Kelowna and Kamloops hold a dispute rate under 3%.

Positioning: "Your parcel rides with someone already going. Same day, door to door, half of express."

8Risks and Controls

Package handling (first/last mile)Handover points on the driver's route, QR codes, small detour budget, 60 kg cap
No repeat sendersTarget marketplace sellers, students and businesses; track repeat rate weekly
Insurance and ICBC validityPlatform master policy + business-use rate class switch at onboarding; no trip without verified coverage
Illegal or dangerous cargoProhibited items list, declaration, inspect-and-refuse right, report flow (operations manual)

No-go trigger

If after 90 days there are fewer than 150 delivered parcels or repeat senders are under 15%, stop and rework the model rather than adding corridors. The pilot exists to kill bad assumptions cheaply.

10Verdict

Proceed - as a lean three-corridor BC pilot

The three corridors are legally free intra-provincially, need no operating authority, and fit a BC company's first market. Combined Year 3 contribution volume is about $464k, which is a real pilot but not a company - the objective is proving repeat demand and the handover playbook before replicating in Alberta and Ontario. Launch Kelowna first, Kamloops on the same Coquihalla driver pool second, Victoria third.
