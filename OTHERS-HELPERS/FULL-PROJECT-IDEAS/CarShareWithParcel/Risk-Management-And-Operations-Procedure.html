1Purpose and Design Principles

This manual protects three interests at once: the sender's parcel, the carrier's freedom from legal and financial risk, and the platform's survival. Every rule below is built on five principles.

  1. The carrier must never be surprised by what is in the box. Listing questions, declaration rules and the right to inspect-and-refuse exist so a carrier never unknowingly transports something illegal or dangerous.
  2. Money never moves without evidence. Escrow holds the contribution until the delivery code plus photo plus GPS confirmation exist. No evidence, no payout - this single rule stops most theft and fraud.
  3. Small parcels only, low declared value. Size bands and value caps keep the criminal interest low: nobody smuggles a meaningful quantity of anything in a 15 kg box with verified identity on both ends.
  4. Refuse first, resolve later. Both parties have a no-penalty refusal right at pickup. A refused trip is always cheaper than a bad trip.
  5. Paper trail on everything. Chat, photos, codes and GPS are recorded automatically. Every dispute is decided by the record, not by he-said-she-said.

3Protection Architecture - 6 Layers

Layer 1 - Identity and Onboarding Before anything

  • Sender and carrier both verify: phone number, government ID, live selfie match, email.
  • Carrier additionally: driving licence check, vehicle model and plate, minimum age, rating floor for high-value parcels.
  • One verified identity = one account. Duplicate accounts or ID reuse are blocked.
  • New accounts have lower value and size limits that rise with completed trips and rating.

Layer 2 - Listing Controls Before the parcel exists

  • Mandatory declaration: what category the parcel is, weight and size band, declared value (capped).
  • Prohibited and restricted lists shown and acknowledged at listing time - not buried in terms.
  • Battery and liquids questions force the dangerous-goods path or block the listing.
  • No cash, no jewelry, no identity documents, no live animals, no perishables above short local limits.

Layer 3 - Matching and Payment Before the trip starts

  • Payment in escrow when the carrier accepts; contribution is cost-sharing capped, plus optional insurance add-on.
  • Route, time window and handover points agreed in-app; no off-platform deals (off-platform = no protection, and accounts are warned).
  • Optional insurance decision happens at listing, never after an incident.

Layer 4 - Pickup Handover The critical 60 seconds

  • Both parties scan a one-time QR code shown in the app - this confirms the right people are present.
  • Carrier performs the inspect-and-refuse check (weight, shape, sound, odor) and may refuse with no penalty.
  • Photo of the sealed parcel taken in-app before loading; parcel goes directly into the trunk.

Layer 5 - In Transit While moving

  • GPS tracking shared with the sender; expected delivery window updated automatically.
  • Parcel stays sealed; opening a parcel without a support case is a violation.
  • Stops are fine, but the parcel is never left visible in a parked car for more than a short stop.

Layer 6 - Delivery and Payout The closing evidence

  • Receiver shows the one-time delivery code from the app; without it there is no handover.
  • Delivery photo (parcel at destination) + GPS confirmation + receiver tap completes the trip.
  • Escrow releases to the carrier, both parties rate each other, dispute window opens (48 h).

The declaration is a contract

At listing, the sender declares the category, weight band and value. A false declaration voids insurance, voids the platform's protection, and is grounds for an instant account ban plus a report to authorities where the law requires it.

5Dangerous Goods Quick Guide

  • Always allowed, no declaration needed: books, clothing, documents, household goods, sealed food in unopened original packaging (same-day only).
  • Allowed with declaration and limits: one sealed consumer electronic device per parcel (phone, tablet, laptop, camera) with the battery inside the device; one power bank up to 100 Wh in original packaging; small aerosols up to 150 ml; sealed liquids up to 100 ml per bottle, max 2 bottles.
  • Never allowed: anything leaking, warm or swollen batteries, loose batteries without packaging, any liquid over the limits, fuel, paint, gas canisters, sprays under pressure larger than 150 ml, unknown powders or unlabeled substances.
  • Carrier check at pickup: if the parcel is warm, ticking, leaking, smells of fuel or chemicals, or makes a loose-battery rattle, refuse it and tap "report item" - no penalty, and the platform investigates the sender.

6SOP-1 Sender - List a Parcel

SOP-1 Sender

Who runs it: sender - supervised by the platform automatically.

  1. Declare: pick the category from the list, answer the dangerous-goods questions truthfully, enter weight band, size band and declared value (capped).
  2. Set the route: pickup point (prefer a public meeting point over a home address), destination point, delivery window.
  3. Choose protection: accept the default value cap or add the insurance option (2-4% of declared value).
  4. Pack correctly: rigid box, inner padding, tape all seams, no external labels that advertise the contents.
  5. Pay into escrow: the contribution plus service fee and insurance are held - nothing is released to anyone until delivery evidence exists.
  6. Stay in-app: all chat, photos and codes happen in the app. Off-platform contact voids the protection.

7SOP-2 Carrier - Review and Accept

SOP-2 Carrier

Who runs it: carrier - before accepting any trip.

  1. Check the listing: category, weight band, value, route and window fit your actual trip - never accept a parcel you cannot deliver in the window.
  2. Check the sender: verified badge, rating, trip history. New accounts with high-value items are a warning signal.
  3. Accept only if genuine: your trip must be real. Declining is always fine and never penalized.
  4. Re-read the prohibited list: if the declaration says "documents" but the parcel feels like bottles, you will refuse at pickup - the declaration is what you agreed to.
  5. Keep it in-app: the pickup code, chat and address stay inside the app. If anyone asks to move off-platform, report and decline.

8SOP-3 Pickup Handover

SOP-3 Both parties

Who runs it: sender and carrier together - this is the highest-risk moment of the whole trip.

  1. Meet at the agreed public point - daylight where possible, camera-covered area preferred.
  2. Scan the one-time QR codes: sender shows theirs, carrier shows theirs, both confirm in-app. Mismatch = no handover, report to platform.
  3. Carrier inspects without opening: weight, shape, sound, odor. Any mismatch with the declaration, any leak, rattle, heat or chemical smell = refuse via "report item" with no penalty.
  4. Photo of the sealed parcel taken in-app by the carrier, timestamped and GPS-tagged.
  5. Trunk-to-trunk only: parcel goes into the trunk (or a closed cargo area), never onto a seat, never visible.
  6. Both tap "parcel in car" - tracking starts for the sender, escrow confirms, the trip is live.

9SOP-4 In Transit

SOP-4 Carrier

Who runs it: carrier - with automatic platform monitoring.

  1. Drive the agreed route - the trip is the one you listed; no detours that change the delivery window without notifying the sender.
  2. Parcel stays sealed and in the trunk. Opening a parcel without a support case is a violation and voids your protection.
  3. Short stops only: if you leave the car, the parcel must not be visible; never leave it in a parked car for extended periods.
  4. If anything goes wrong (accident, breakdown, police stop, theft) - tap "report incident" immediately, message the sender, and follow SOP-6.
  5. Update the ETA if delayed more than 30 minutes; silence is what creates disputes.

10SOP-5 Delivery and Payout

SOP-5 Both parties

Who runs it: carrier and receiver together - the trip closes only with evidence.

  1. Meet at the agreed destination point.
  2. Receiver shows the one-time delivery code from the app - no code, no handover, no exceptions.
  3. Carrier takes the delivery photo (parcel at the destination, in-app, GPS-tagged) and the receiver taps "received".
  4. Escrow releases automatically to the carrier after the receiver's tap (or after the 48 h auto-release if the receiver is unresponsive).
  5. Both parties rate each other. The 48-hour dispute window opens now - evidence is already saved.

11SOP-6 Incident and Dispute Handling

SOP-6 Platform support

Who runs it: platform - with fixed time boxes so nobody waits in the dark.

  1. Triage (under 2 h): every report gets a case number and an acknowledgment. Escrow stays frozen on that trip.
  2. Evidence pull (under 24 h): support collects the automatic record - chat, QR scans, pickup and delivery photos, GPS, delivery code state.
  3. Decision (under 5 days): support rules on the record. Damage or loss with insurance = claim paid to sender, carrier fault reviewed separately. Dispute without evidence = auto-release in favor of the completed delivery code.
  4. Appeal (under 10 days): either party may appeal once with new evidence; the decision after appeal is final.
  5. Pattern review: any party involved in 3 disputes in 90 days is reviewed, and may lose limits or the account.

12SOP-7 Illegal or Dangerous Cargo Found

SOP-7 Both parties + platform

Who runs it: whoever discovers it - with a strict sequence so the innocent party is never at risk.

  1. Do not open it further. If you suspect illegal or dangerous contents at pickup - refuse before it enters your car and tap "report item".
  2. If discovered later: pull over safely, do not handle the item, tap the emergency "report dangerous cargo" button in the app.
  3. Platform freezes both accounts temporarily and contacts the carrier with instructions within 15 minutes (target).
  4. Never dispose of it yourself. The platform coordinates safe handover to authorities. Dumping a suspicious item can turn a witness into a suspect.
  5. Contact authorities through the platform flow: the carrier makes the call to police with platform guidance; the report is logged in the case record for the carrier's protection.
  6. Innocent-party protection: the reporting party keeps their account, rating and earnings for the trip; the sender side is investigated, banned and reported where the law requires.

Golden rule

A carrier who honestly reports suspicious cargo is protected. A carrier who quietly drops it off and hopes for the best is not. Refuse or report - never ignore.

13SOP-8 Fraud and Security Incidents

SOP-8 Platform security team

  1. Impersonation attempt: QR mismatch at handover = trip cancelled, escrow returned, both real users notified, flagged account reviewed.
  2. Off-platform solicitation: first offense = warning and protection loss on that trip; repeat = account ban. Off-platform deals are where fraud lives.
  3. Stolen parcel reported by carrier: police report required, insurance claim opened, sender compensated under the insurance terms, carrier's account paused until the report is verified.
  4. False non-delivery claim by sender: delivery code + photo + GPS decides; confirmed false claims = claim dismissed, sender warned or banned.
  5. Suspicious repeated behavior: same phone number, new accounts from one device, identical parcel patterns - automatic flagging and manual review.

14SOP-9 Emergency and Personal Safety

SOP-9 Anyone, anytime

  1. Emergency button in the app: one tap shares live location with a trusted contact and logs the event to the platform.
  2. Meeting rules: public places, daylight, cameras. If the meeting point feels unsafe, leave and report - no penalty ever for walking away from a bad handover.
  3. Road emergency: accident or breakdown - report in-app, the sender sees the status, the trip pauses safely, no penalties for delays caused by emergencies.
  4. Threat during a trip: dial local emergency services first, then tap the app emergency button. Platform support takes over the case afterward.
  5. Never carry unplanned cargo: if someone asks to add items not listed in the app at pickup - refuse. Undocumented cargo is the number one way illegal items get in.

15Escrow and Payment Protection

  • Sender protection: money stays in escrow until the delivery code + photo + GPS confirm delivery. No delivery = full refund, and the insurance decision was made at listing.
  • Carrier protection: payout is automatic once the evidence exists - a receiver who disappears cannot trap the money; auto-release after 48 h with evidence, or after the dispute resolution.
  • Refusal protection: refusing a parcel at pickup (SOP-3) releases the escrow back to the sender within minutes, and the carrier's rating is untouched.
  • Cancellation rules: sender cancels before pickup = full refund. Carrier cancels after accepting = sender refunded, carrier gets a cancellation mark; repeated cancellation marks reduce matching priority.
  • Chargebacks: paid via app wallet or card with the evidence pack attached to every chargeback response - the record wins most disputes.

16Insurance Design and Claims

What the optional add-on covers

  • Covered: loss of the parcel while in the carrier's custody (theft from the car with police report, or disappearance), and damage from an accident or handling.
  • Value cap: default coverage up to the standard cap (example: 250 EUR); declared-value upgrade available at listing with a receipt.
  • Not covered: prohibited items, cash, jewelry, identity documents, perishables outside rules, parcels that were refused-then-accepted off-app, and any trip where the declaration was false.
  • Cost: 2-4% of declared value, paid by the sender at listing - never sold after an incident (that is how fraud happens).

Claim flow

  1. Incident reported in-app within 24 h (theft: attach the police report within 72 h).
  2. Evidence pack assembled automatically (photos, GPS, chat, codes).
  3. Claim decision within 5 working days; payout to the sender's wallet or bank on approval.
  4. Carrier liability reviewed separately - negligence is addressed by warnings, limits or bans, not by billing the carrier personally (keeps carriers honest and willing to report).

19Evidence Standards

Every dispute is decided by the record. The platform captures automatically, with timestamps and GPS:

  • QR scans at pickup and delivery code at handover
  • Photos of the sealed parcel at pickup and delivery
  • Full in-app chat between the parties
  • Route and GPS track during the trip
  • Escrow, refund and payout events
  • Report buttons with the reporting party's identity and time

Parties may add their own evidence (extra photos, police report, receipts). Evidence is kept for the claims window plus the legal retention period, then deleted under the data policy.

20First 90 Days Safety Checklist

  1. Prohibited list + dangerous goods guide live in the app at listing, with forced acknowledgment.
  2. ID verification and selfie match working for both roles before the first live trip.
  3. Escrow flow tested end-to-end including refusal, cancellation, auto-release and dispute freeze.
  4. QR pickup + delivery codes tested on real devices with mismatch cases.
  5. Insurance add-on activated with the value cap and exclusions displayed.
  6. Emergency and report buttons wired to a real on-call person, not a mailbox.
  7. Public handover point guidance shown at every listing and acceptance.
  8. Support SLAs staffed - Tier 1 under 2 h, dangerous cargo line under 15 min.
  9. Jurisdiction structure in place per the regulation guide before opening each corridor.
  10. Weekly safety review meeting - every report, dispute and refusal reviewed; rules updated if a pattern appears.
