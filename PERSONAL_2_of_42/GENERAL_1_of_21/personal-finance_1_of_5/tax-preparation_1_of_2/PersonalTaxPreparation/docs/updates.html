2. Highlights

v0.1.3 - See the whole tool before you start. The marketing page now shows a full picture tour - nine real screenshots of every page, from the dashboard to the GST/PST summary and the reports, instead of a live demo. The help page shows what each step looks like, and the social share kit attaches the same screenshots. All pictures use sample data - your numbers never appear.

v0.1.1 - Document kind areas. The Documents page now has four dedicated areas - bank statements, invoices paid, invoices sent and other documents - each with its own upload button, guidance and count. The AI now knows what each document is for, so invoices paid become expenses and invoices sent become income automatically. Existing documents were migrated without losing anything.
