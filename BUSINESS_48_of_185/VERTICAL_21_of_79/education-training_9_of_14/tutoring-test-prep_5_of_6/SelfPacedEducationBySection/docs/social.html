5. One-pager pitch

Self-Paced Education is a UniconHub html-tool that turns a Curriculum Builder course into a personal study journey: students unlock sections progressively, finish lessons with quizzes and reviews, and follow a recommended target end date - while supervisors watch live progress and share status reports in 9 languages via WhatsApp or email. One CMS record holds one student's full journey. The differentiator: reports are status-aware (cheering matches the real progress) and the shared link always points to the exact course page - with zero per-student setup.
