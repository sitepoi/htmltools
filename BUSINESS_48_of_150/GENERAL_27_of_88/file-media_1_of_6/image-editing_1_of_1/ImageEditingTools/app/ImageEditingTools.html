File type converter

Opens any image the browser supports (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, BMP, SVG…) and saves it as PNG, JPEG, WebP or AVIF (where supported). Animated GIFs keep their first frame. HEIC (iPhone) files need a converter first — most browsers can't open them.

Resize

Change the pixel dimensions. The aspect ratio lock keeps the image from stretching.

Crop

Drag on the preview to select an area, type exact numbers, or use a social-size preset. Circle crop turns it into an avatar.

Rotate & flip

Quarter turns and mirror flips apply instantly. Fine rotation can tilt the image by any angle.

Drag across the horizon on the preview — release to level the image. Esc cancels.

Frame & border studio

Wrap the image in a border, mat and shadow. The Polaroid preset adds a white paper frame with a caption.

Filters & adjustments

The preview updates live as you move the sliders. Click "Apply to image" to make it permanent. Grain is baked on Apply.

Annotate

Draw arrows, shapes and highlights directly on the preview. Marks stay editable until you bake them into the image.

Batch processing

Run the same pipeline over many files at once and download them as a ZIP. Files never leave your browser.

Compress

Shrink the file size for email, chat or the web. Set a target size and let the auto-tuner find the best quality.

Text watermark

Stamp text over the image — a name, a logo word, a date or a "draft" notice. Change your mind? Just Undo.

Photo collage

Combine several images into one frame. Drop images on the slot boxes (or drop them on the preview while this tab is open).

OCR — text from image

Load a photo or scan of a document and pull out its text. The built-in engine runs fully in the browser; the AI engine works through the CMS AI gateway.
