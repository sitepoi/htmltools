Essay Writing Educator - Feature Ideas & Roadmap

A description document and idea list for the UniconHub CMS html-tool. Importance is scored out of 10, benefits and difficulty are explained for every idea.

What the tool is today

The Essay Writing Educator is a single-essay-per-student html-tool. A teacher or CMS admin defines the essay subject and instructions through field parameters. The student writes the essay in a centered, page-like rich text editor (paragraph styles, bold, italic, font size). An evaluation service scores the essay against weighted rubrics and returns a score out of 100 with extremely detailed feedback: an overall summary, strengths, weaknesses, per-rubric scores, comments and prioritized improvement suggestions. The evaluation service never rewrites or edits the student text - it only suggests.

  • Five base rubrics (Content & Ideas 30%, Organization 20%, Evidence 20%, Language 15%, Grammar 15%) plus teacher-defined custom rubrics, weights must total 100%.
  • Rubrics and Evaluation open as animated right-side panels; the editor slides left when they open.
  • A compact score chip in the top bar shows the latest score and opens the full evaluation.
  • Feedback tab: version dropdown (score badge + version number + date), grade band, score ring, rubric breakdown, strengths, weaknesses, next steps, copy-all-suggestions.
  • Progress tab: overall score line chart and per-rubric line chart showing improvement between versions.
  • Evaluation history is stored in the record value; teacher-only rubric management.
