What is this page?

This is the setup of the current school year of this school. Everything you configure here - classes, payment brackets, lessons, the school calendar and folder ids - belongs to this school year only. When a new school year starts, create a new School Setup object inside that year's 0-Setup folder.

What are payment brackets?

Payment brackets are the pricing plans of this school year. Each bracket has a monthly fee and an optional discounted monthly fee. When a bracket is assigned to a student, its fee becomes that student's monthly installment. Because this School Setup object lives in the school year's 0-Setup folder, brackets are automatically scoped to this school year - next year's setup gets its own brackets.

One-time payments

One-time payments are not monthly installments - for example a registration fee for the whole school, or a class trip fee for one class. School-level fees apply to every student; class-level fees apply to the students of the selected class. Event-based fees (trips, events) belong to a separate tool, not here.

Why are lessons managed here?

Every lesson is a subject taught at the school - for example the subject Quran with its levels Level 1 and Level 2. Students belong to a class (see the Classes tab) and attend one or more lessons. The lesson also carries the teacher, schedule, progress checklist, its attendance and progress subfolders, and its attendance requirement - so the other school tools (attendance, progress, reports) reuse it as is.

What are classes?

Classes are the groups students belong to - for example Grade 1-A, Grade 1-B, Grade 3-4, or flower names. They are separate from lessons: a student sits in one class but attends several lessons. Define the class names here, then assign each student to a class in the student record tool.

What are teachers?

Teachers are the real users of this application. Record their name and email here - the email is their login identity. Then assign teachers to lessons and classes from these lists. In the application's permission settings, grant the same email access to the teacher's lesson folders so they only see their own lessons.

How does the school calendar work?

You build an explicit list of school days. Add a recurring weekly pattern (one day per week, several days per week, or every second week), then add or remove single dates for weeks that differ - a different day in one week, an extra session, or a make-up day. Attendance tools use exactly this list.

What are folder ids?

All school tools live inside the same application. The folders below are folders of that application, and the folder ids are the connection between them: students are stored in the Students folder, lesson objects in the Lessons folder, payment ledgers in the Payments folder and so on. Set them once per year - teachers and other tools read them automatically and never need to type them.

Changing an id later can disconnect data: objects already created keep living in the old folder, and the tools would start reading the new folder instead. When you save changed ids, you will be asked to confirm.
