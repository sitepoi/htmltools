The problem

Volunteer coordination lives in spreadsheets and email

Every event re-enters the same volunteer list, approvals happen in reply-all threads, and hour totals are tallied by hand at the end.

  • The same volunteers are re-entered for every event
  • Applications get lost in email threads
  • Hours are tallied manually, and certificates are never issued
The solution

One shared registry, one tool per event

Volunteer Management sits on the event page: volunteers apply, managers approve and assign, hours add up, and certificates come out at the end.

Volunteers know where they stand

One status card shows their application, their tasks and their approved hours.

Managers see everything

A dashboard with pending approvals, active tasks and hour totals in real time.

Why it matters

Less admin, more event

No re-entry

Profiles live once in the shared registry.

No lost applications

Pending approvals wait on the dashboard, not in an inbox.

No manual tallies

Approved hours total themselves per volunteer.

How it works

From signup to certificate in three steps

1 · Volunteers register

People with event access apply with skills and availability.

2 · Managers approve and assign

Approve applications, create tasks with due dates and assignees.

3 · Hours become certificates

Volunteers log hours, managers approve, certificates are generated.
