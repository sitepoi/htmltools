2. Highlights

Volunteer Management now runs on a shared volunteer registry. Volunteer profiles live once in the registry, and every event adds only its own applications, tasks, hours and messages - so a volunteer who helped at one event is one click away at the next. Managers can approve applications with one click, add people directly from the permitted users list, and issue certificates with total approved hours at the end of the event.

The CSV import companion tool now writes into the same registry with email deduplication, so bulk-loaded volunteer lists never create duplicates.
