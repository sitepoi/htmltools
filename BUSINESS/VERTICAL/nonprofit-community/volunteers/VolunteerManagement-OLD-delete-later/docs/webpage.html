Questions before you start

Is this one tool for all my events?

Each event gets its own tool instance, so its volunteers, tasks and hours stay separate. The volunteer registry is shared, so a volunteer who helped before is already known at your next event.

Can volunteers apply by themselves?

Yes - anyone with access to the event page can submit a registration. Managers approve or reject each application.

What do volunteers get at the end?

A volunteer hours certificate with their total approved hours and completed tasks, downloadable as PDF or sent by email.
