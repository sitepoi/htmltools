The problem

Revenue-based pricing was being invoiced by hand

Every month someone re-read the agreement and recalculated the fee in a spreadsheet - and the agreement itself was open to interpretation.

  • Rates applied inconsistently across months
  • Caps and the negotiated tier easy to miss
  • Customers could not see how their total was reached
The solution

One deterministic waterfall engine

Revenue Based Invoice Builder turns the agreement into code: each rate applies only to the revenue inside its bracket, caps win, and the math prints on every invoice.

Zero interpretation

The same brackets and caps compute every month, rounded to the cent.

Zero surprises

Issued amounts freeze with a pricing snapshot; later agreement changes never touch them.
