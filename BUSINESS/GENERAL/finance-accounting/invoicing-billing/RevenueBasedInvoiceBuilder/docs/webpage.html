Questions before you start

How does the waterfall actually work?

Each rate applies only to the revenue inside its bracket. A CA $160,000 month pays CA $300 minimum on the first $20,000, plus CA $550 (1.1% bracket, capped) and CA $680 (0.8% bracket) - CA $1,530 total.

What happens above CA $3,000,000?

The standard brackets apply up to $3,000,000 and the invoice requires the agreed negotiated fee for the remainder before it can be saved.

Can invoices carry extra work beyond the subscription?

Yes - every invoice supports additional lines for custom work, one-time integrations or new agreements, plus a selectable support type with its own monthly fee.
