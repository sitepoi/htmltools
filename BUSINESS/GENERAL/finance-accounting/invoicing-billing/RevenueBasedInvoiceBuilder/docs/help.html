Revenue Based Invoice Builder - Help

User help for Revenue Based Invoice Builder. The single source of truth for how this tool works is its SSOT document (SSOT) - this file explains the same behavior for end users, in plain words. Generated from the tool code + SSOT + AI chat by scripts/generate-docs.js. Updated: 2026-09-30.

1. Overview

Revenue Based Invoice Builder creates the monthly invoices for a software product whose price is based on the customer's monthly ticket revenue.

Billing teams use it to turn each month's revenue into a correct, explainable invoice: the tool applies the pricing agreement's waterfall brackets, shows the math while you type, and freezes issued amounts forever.

2. Before you start

  • Editors can create invoices and manage customers; only admins can edit the default pricing agreement, delete customers or invoices, or reset data.
  • One tool instance is one vendor record with many customers - add them in the Customers tab.
  • Fill Settings first: your company details, currency, tax rate, invoice prefix and the default customer.

3. Step by step

  1. Add your customers in the Customers tab - each one uses the default waterfall unless you give them their own rates.
  2. In Settings, define your support types (name + optional monthly fee), pick the default, and upload your company logo.
  3. Click + New Invoice, pick the customer, the support type and the billing month.
  4. Enter the monthly ticket revenue - the waterfall breakdown updates on every keystroke using that customer's rates.
  5. Add additional lines for custom work, integrations or new agreements from this month.
  6. Check the breakdown, tax and due date, then click Save Invoice.
  7. Open the invoice to Print / PDF it or Email it to the customer.
  8. Mark it sent, then paid when the money arrives.

4. Common questions

QuestionAnswer
Why does a CA $160,000 month invoice CA $1,530?Waterfall: CA $300 minimum on the first $20,000 + CA $550 (1.1% on $20k-$75k, capped) + CA $680 (0.8% on $75k-$160k).
Which waterfall does an invoice use?The selected customer's own waterfall when they have one, otherwise the default Pricing Agreement.
How do I add extra work to an invoice?Use the Additional lines box on the invoice: a description and an amount per line (integrations, custom work, one-time agreements).
How does the support type work?Each support type has a name and an optional monthly fee. The invoice uses the default from Settings unless you pick another one; the fee is added as its own line.
Why does my printed invoice only show some bracket rows?Only the brackets that actually contributed to the fee are printed - zero rows are omitted so the invoice stays clean. The in-app breakdown still shows the full waterfall.
How do I get my logo on invoices?Settings > Company logo > Upload. The field needs allowUpload: yes in the CMS field settings; the logo then appears on printed and emailed invoices.
How do I give one customer different rates?Open the customer in the Customers tab, uncheck "Use the default waterfall" and edit their brackets (or copy the default first).
What happens when revenue is above CA $3,000,000?The standard brackets apply up to $3,000,000 and the form asks for the negotiated fee for the rest - you cannot save without it.
Can a rate change later?Admins edit the default waterfall in the Pricing Agreement tab and editors edit per-customer waterfalls; already saved invoices keep the amounts they were issued with.
Is tax included?Each invoice has its own tax rate (0 by default). Tax is applied to the subtotal (subscription + support + extra lines) and shown as its own line.

5. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
New Invoice says to add a customerOpen the Customers tab and add at least one customer first.
Logo upload says uploads are not enabledSet allowUpload: yes on the html-tool field in the CMS Field Group Designer.
Email button warns the customer has no emailOpen the customer in the Customers tab and fill their email, then try again.
The total shows "Enter the negotiated fee"Revenue is above CA $3,000,000 - enter the agreed fee in the highlighted box.
Changes do not seem to saveIf a warning about autosave appeared, enable allowRequestSave in the field settings; the value is still staged.
