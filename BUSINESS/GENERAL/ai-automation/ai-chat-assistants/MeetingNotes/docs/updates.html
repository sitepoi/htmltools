2. Highlights

Cleaner subjects and friendlier Meeting Mode (2026-09-29): The Subjects tab is now a pure reading list - edit cards are gone, the list folds with Collapse all / Expand all, and adding a subject no longer reshuffles the view. All editing happens in Meeting Mode, which also gained a "+ New subject" button, labelled toolbar controls and Tab / Shift+Tab indentation for nested list items.

Mobile-first refresh (2026-09-28): Meeting Notes now fits phones end to end - finger-sized controls, stacked cards, a slide-in subjects drawer in Meeting Mode and a fully scrollable page. The subject model got simpler: no decisions layer, just notes, status and time. In Meeting Mode the timer lives in the right panel above the tasks, and the big number switches between the countdown (while it runs) and the spent time (when it is not running).

First release (2026-09-28): Meeting Notes arrived as a complete boardroom workspace with agenda subjects, a live countdown, action items with owners, AI-drafted minutes and six export formats.
