Questions you may have

Is my meeting data saved automatically?

Yes - every change autosaves within a second, including a final save when you close the page. There is no Save button to forget.

Can viewers accidentally change the meeting?

No. People with viewer access see everything but all editing controls are disabled or hidden for them.

What if our CMS does not provide the assignee list?

The assignee fields simply become free text, so tasks keep working exactly the same.
