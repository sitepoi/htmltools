The problem

Meetings run long and minutes are written later

Agendas live in one place, notes in notebooks, and action items in people's heads - nobody knows how long each topic really took.

  • No per-topic time pressure - one item eats the whole meeting
  • Minutes written after the fact miss decisions and motions
  • Verbal follow-ups get forgotten
The solution

One structured, time-aware meeting document

Meeting Notes keeps the agenda, decisions, tasks and timing in one autosaved workspace, and drafts the documents for you.
