Policy Acceptance

Employee Attendance Policy

Schedule Expectations: Employees are expected to follow their assigned work schedule. Your regular schedule defines your working days, start time, and end time.

Punctuality: Arrive on time for every scheduled shift. Late arrivals must be communicated to your supervisor as early as possible.

Communication: Any schedule changes must be requested through the formal permission system before the change occurs. Verbal approvals are not valid.

Permission Process: Submit a request through this system. Your supervisor will review and provide a formal decision. You will be notified of the outcome.

Unpaid Approved Absence: Time away from work that is approved but not worked is generally unpaid unless your supervisor explicitly approves paid status.

No Guaranteed Make-Up Hours: Working additional hours outside your regular schedule to "make up" for missed time is not automatically permitted and requires separate approval.

Repeated Requests: Frequent schedule change requests may trigger additional review to ensure fairness and operational consistency.

Educational Sessions: Required training and educational sessions cannot be missed without explicit administrator approval.

Supervisor Management Policy

Consistency Requirement: All employees must be treated fairly and consistently. Apply the same standards to every team member.

Documentation Requirement: Every decision must be documented with reasons. No informal or verbal exceptions.

Fair Treatment: Do not create different rules for different employees. All schedule exceptions follow the same process.

No Unofficial Exceptions: All schedule changes must go through this formal system. Side agreements are not permitted.

Escalation Requirements: Escalate when: an employee has more than 3 requests in 30 days, requests affect educational sessions, or a pattern of repeated late arrivals emerges.

Payment Decisions: Clearly indicate whether approved time is paid or unpaid. Default is unpaid for time not worked.
